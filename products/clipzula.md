# Clipzula

AI turns live streams and long videos into viral shorts

- **Site:** [clipzula.com](https://www.clipzula.com/)
- **Topic:** [Visual and media](../topics/visual-and-media.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-27

Clipzula turns long videos, YouTube links and live Twitch/Kick streams into vertical clips with captions, titles and hashtags, and posts them to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram. Paid plans clip while you are still live. Free: 60 min a month, no card.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Can you buy a single month of Clipzula without a recurring subscription?

Yes, creators can buy one month with a single payment without signing up for a subscription. The access lasts for 30 days and nothing automatically renews.

> Buy one month with a single payment: Starter $12, Standard $24, Advanced $65. Nothing renews.

### Can I upload MKV recordings, and how big can the file be?

Yes. Uploads can be MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM, up to 2 GB and 3 hours. On paid plans a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link can run up to 12 hours, while the free plan stops at 60 minutes.

> MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM up to 2 GB and 3 hours

### How many social accounts can Clipzula post to for me?

Three on Starter and Standard, ten on Advanced. Advanced also allows 5 posts per platform a day instead of 3, and the free plan has no auto posting.

> Auto posting: 10 accounts

### What resolution and frame rate do the clips come out in?

The same as your source video. Clips keep the resolution and frame rate of the video you give Clipzula.

> same as deliverd video

### Can it clip a YouTube live stream while I'm streaming?

No. Live clipping works with Twitch and Kick channels only. You can still paste a YouTube link to a finished video and get clips from that.

> live clipping is currently restricted to Twitch and Kick channels

### Is live clipping included on the free plan?

No. Live clipping starts on the paid plans. Starter and Standard follow up to 3 channels and clip one stream at a time.

> Paid plans clip while you are still live.

### How long can a video link be?

Up to 12 hours on paid plans, so a full stream fits in one go.

> Paid plans take links up to 12 hours, so a full stream fits in one go.

### Can I choose how long the clips are?

Yes. Let it decide, or pick under 30 seconds, 30 to 60 seconds or 60 to 90 seconds when a campaign asks for a length.

> Let Clipzula decide, or choose under 30 seconds, 30-60 seconds or 60-90 seconds when a campaign asks for a length.

### Is it connected to Whop Content Rewards?

No. You make the clips in Clipzula, then post them and submit them to the campaign yourself.

> It is not connected to Whop: you make the clips here and post and submit them yourself.

### Which video files can I upload?

MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM, up to 2 GB and 3 hours per upload. Longer streams can go in as a link on paid plans.

> MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM up to 2 GB and 3 hours

### Do the prices include VAT?

Yes. Prices are in USD and already include VAT.

> Prices in USD, incl. VAT.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't clip live streams outside Twitch and Kick.

  > Live clipping for Twitch & Kick

- It doesn't import straight from Zoom, StreamYard or Riverside; you upload the file or paste a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link.

  > OpusClip supports more import sources such as Zoom, StreamYard and Riverside.

- It doesn't clip while you are live on the free plan; live clipping is for paid plans.

  > Paid plans clip while you are still live.

- It doesn't take videos longer than 60 minutes on the free plan.

  > Videos up to 60 minutes

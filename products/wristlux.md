# WristLux

A light meter for quick lux checks from your Wear OS watch

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.wristlux](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.wristlux)
- **Topic:** [Desktop and utilities](../topics/desktop-and-utilities.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-21

WristLux uses a supported watch ambient-light sensor for quick lux and foot-candle checks. Track min, max and average readings, hold a value, calibrate, and glance at the latest cached reading from a Tile or complication.

### Does WristLux keep statistics for a measuring session?

Yes. It tracks the minimum, maximum, average and duration of each session.

### Why does the reading change when I move my arm?

Because the sensor sits on your wrist. Readings vary with watch model, wrist angle, shadows, cases, sleeves and sensor placement, so hold still and keep the same pose.

> Ambient light readings are approximate and vary by watch model, wrist angle, shadows, cases, sleeves, and sensor placement.

### Is it good enough for professional photo or lighting work?

No. It is a quick reference for scouting and everyday checks, not a certified professional light meter.

> WristLux is a quick light reference, not a certified professional light meter.

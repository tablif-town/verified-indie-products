# Flexlio 3.0

Build, sync and export editable banner campaigns in Figma

- **Site:** [flexlio.co.uk](https://flexlio.co.uk/)
- **Topic:** [Marketing and sales](../topics/marketing-and-sales.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-15

Big campaign. Endless sizes. “Just one more amend.” Meet Flexlio. Turn approved Figma masters into editable banner campaigns, then sync changes across your sizes. Your design decisions carry through. Every frame stays yours to tweak. New in 3.0: master carousels, Excel media plan imports and batch layered PSD exports for retouching and motion. Plus PNG exports, safe zones and artwork checks. Built by a designer who’s done the late nights. Get your campaign out the door.

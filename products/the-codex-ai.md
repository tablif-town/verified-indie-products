# The Codex AI

Immersive multimodal AI storytelling & roleplay sandbox

- **Site:** [thecodexai.com](https://thecodexai.com/)
- **Topic:** [AI and agents](../topics/ai-and-agents.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-19

The Codex AI is an immersive interactive storytelling engine where your choices shape living, branching worlds. Beyond generic text chatbots with amnesia, The Codex AI brings: • CYOA, Director, and Roleplay modes • Deep world memory & persistent lorebooks

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Is there an Android app?

Yes, but it is in closed testing, so your Google account has to join the testers program before it installs.

> It is still in closed testing, so your Google account has to join the testers program before the store listing will install anything

### Can I import my SillyTavern or Chub character cards?

Yes. You can import SillyTavern and Chub PNG cards, JSON cards and whole collections with drag and drop in the browser.

> 1-click browser import for SillyTavern / Chub PNG character cards

### How much do the paid memberships cost?

Supporter is $5 a month, Adventurer $10 a month and Legend $20 a month. They add more zero-key models and remove ads.

> upgrade to a Supporter ($5/mo), Adventurer ($10/mo), or Legend ($20/mo) membership

### Is The Codex AI free to use?

Yes. The core storytelling and roleplay features are free, and paid memberships add more curated models and remove ads.

> The Codex AI provides 100% free access to all core storytelling and roleplay features

### Do I need my own API key to start?

No. The Free tier comes with a platform-provided model, DeepSeek V4 Flash 0423, so you can play without setting up a key.

> play free on the Free tier with a platform-provided curated model (DeepSeek V4 Flash 0423 — no API key setup)

### Which providers can I connect with my own key?

Many, including OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek, xAI, Mistral, NovelAI and AI Horde.

> Direct browser-to-LLM streaming with 18+ providers — OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek, xAI, Mistral, NovelAI, AI Horde, or fully local Ollama and LM Studio.

### Can I run it with a local model?

Yes. Local setups such as Ollama and LM Studio are supported.

> or fully local Ollama and LM Studio

### Can I turn a story into an eBook?

Yes. Chronicle Studio is a novel authoring mode with EPUB and PDF export.

> Dedicated novel authoring studio with real-time AI scene commentary, manuscript contribution highlights, and EPUB/PDF export.

### Can characters speak out loud?

Yes. OmniResonance gives characters synthesized voices, with more than 30 neural voice presets.

> High-fidelity character voice synthesis with 30+ neural presets

### Can several characters share one chat?

Yes. Cast Lounges are multi-character chat rooms with presence status and in-line mentions.

> Assemble multi-character bot chat rooms with live character presence

### How are my API keys protected?

Keys are encrypted on your side before use, and the Aegis Vault uses AES-256-GCM encryption.

> client-encrypted API keys

### Are there daily message limits on the Free tier?

No. The founder says there are no daily message limits; image generation on the Free tier is capped at 10 Elysium images a month.

> No daily message limits. 10 Elysium AI Images/Mo

### Can the team read my chats?

Not once you set up your end-to-end passphrase. Chats are encrypted before they are stored, and the founder says even database admins cannot open them.

> Not even the database admins can access your chats as long as you've setup your E2EE passphrase.

### Can I branch a story and try a different choice?

Yes. ChronoNexus shows your story as a 2D flowchart, so you can split the timeline and follow another path.

> Visual 2D flowchart narrative tree with Quantum Leap and timeline divergence.

### Can I adjust temperature and other model settings?

Yes. You can set Temperature, Top-P, Top-K, Repetition Penalty, Min-P and Top-A, and write your own system prompts.

> Fine-tune Temperature, Top-P, Top-K, Repetition Penalty, Min-P, and Top-A.

### Can I make pictures of my characters?

Yes. Scriptorium creates character portraits and illustrations with several image engines. The Free tier includes 10 Elysium images a month.

> Create high-fidelity anime masterworks, light novel illustrations, and webtoon cast portraits

### Can I bring lorebooks over from other tools?

Yes. It imports SillyTavern World Info files and KoboldAI, NovelAI and Agnaistic lorebooks in batch.

> Import SillyTavern World Info JSONs, KoboldAI, NovelAI, and Agnaistic lorebooks in batch.

### Which slash commands work inside a chat?

Commands such as /ooc, /scene, /pov, /timejump, /whisper, /steer, /branch, /trim and /remember let you steer the story from the chat box.

> /ooc, /scene, /pov, /timejump, /whisper, /steer, /branch, /trim, /remember

### Where do I report a bug?

On the official Discord server, which has a channel for bug reports and live hotfixes with the developers.

> Direct line to submit bug reports, track live hotfixes, and collaborate directly with developers.

### Does the Free tier show ads?

Yes. The Free tier has ads, and any of the three paid memberships (Supporter, Adventurer or Legend) removes them.

> Supporter ($5), Adventurer ($10) and Legend ($20) add cumulative fleets and remove ads.

### Which model do I get on the Free tier?

DeepSeek V4 Flash 0423. The founder confirmed it is the platform-provided model on the Free tier, so you can start chatting without adding a key.

> Q: Is the platform-provided Free tier model currently DeepSeek V4 Flash 0423? A (founder): Yes, it is.

### How many images can I make on the Free tier?

Ten Elysium images a month. Messages have no daily cap, but images are counted monthly.

> No daily message limits. 10 Elysium AI Images/Mo

### Is there a markup on top of what my provider charges?

No. When you connect your own key, requests go from your browser to the provider and the platform adds no token markup, so you pay the provider's own rate.

> Direct browser-to-LLM inference with zero filters, zero prompt censorship, and zero platform token markups.

### What session modes are there?

Three. Story RPG for a classic roleplay, Cast Lounge for group chats with several characters, and Chronicle for writing a novel with the AI.

> Comparing Story RPG, Cast Lounge, and Chronicle Mode authoring with tailored mechanics.

### How does the app track how a character feels about me?

With two scores. Affection runs from -100 to +100 and Trust from 0 to 100, and both shift after each turn with a notice when they change.

> Dual-axis Affection (-100 to +100) and Trust (0 to 100) tracking.

### How does the lorebook pick which entries go into a reply?

It runs three checks each turn. Keyword matching, BM25 relevance scoring and vector embeddings decide which lore and characters get pulled into the prompt.

> Sub-millisecond keyword matching, stemmed BM25 context relevance scoring, and semantic vector embeddings dynamically summon the exact lore and characters needed per turn.

### Does the Android app need store updates to get new features?

No. The app loads the live platform, so a change on the website shows up in the app without updating it from Google Play.

> The app loads the live platform, so every web update reaches it without a store update.

## What it does not do

  > It is still in closed testing, so your Google account has to join the testers program before the store listing will install anything

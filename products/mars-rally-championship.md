# Mars Rally Championship

Free 3D rally racing on Mars, right in your browser

- **Site:** [marsrallychampionship.com](https://marsrallychampionship.com/)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-24

A free 3D rally racing game that runs in your browser: no download, no account, no ads. Drive a pressurized rover across Martian terrain under Mars gravity, chase medal times on six rally stages (forward, reversed and storm boards), race up to 8 friends live, and watch any leaderboard run rebuilt from its recorded inputs. Every board time is re-simulated on the server before it posts.

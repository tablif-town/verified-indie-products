# FeelMyMac

Lets you feel different textures through your Mac’s trackpad

- **Site:** [feelmymac.thehighjack.it](https://feelmymac.thehighjack.it/)
- **Topic:** [Desktop and utilities](../topics/desktop-and-utilities.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-29

FeelMyMac brings haptic textures to cursor movement and scrolling on Mac trackpads. Instead of feeling feedback only when you click, you can actually feel simulated surfaces as you move your cursor, with different materials and textures to choose from. You can enable haptics separately for cursor customize intensity, and add optional sound effects. FeelMyMac also includes keyboard sounds for an extra layer of feedback. How it works? Easy it's Taptic Engine just like an iPhone would!

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### How does FeelMyMac handle my interaction data and privacy?

All enabled interactions are processed locally on your Mac. The app does not collect diagnostics, interaction history, or usage analytics.

> FeelMyMac processes enabled interactions locally to create feedback. We do not receive app-usage analytics, interaction history or diagnostics from the app.

### Is the full license a recurring subscription or a single payment?

The full license requires a single payment for a perpetual license. It includes future software updates without recurring fees.

> One payment. A perpetual license, with future updates included.

### Can I test the local developer API using the free demo?

Local API access is not available in the free demo. Unlocking the API requires purchasing a full license.

> Available in FeelMyMac 1.2.0 and later. API access requires a purchased full license.

### What can I try in the free demo?

Two materials and one sound profile, offline and with no time limit. No email or card is needed.

> Two materials and one sound profile, offline with no time limit. No email or card.

### Which materials can I choose from?

Felt, Bouclé, Pebbled, Fine grit, Soft grid and Line grid, and you can tune roughness and pattern spacing.

> Start with Felt, Bouclé, Pebbled, Fine grit, Soft grid or Line grid.

### Which macOS version do I need?

macOS 15.1 or later.

> macOS 15.1 or later

### Which languages is the app in?

Eight: English, Italian, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

> The app is available in English, Italian, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese.

### Can I adjust the sound without changing the feel?

Yes. Material, click and keyboard sounds have their own settings, and changing them leaves the haptic setup alone.

> Shape material, click and keyboard sounds separately.

### Can I have feedback on scrolling but not on cursor movement?

Yes. Cursor movement, scrolling and taps switch on and off independently, and anything you turn off stops being monitored.

> Enable cursor movement, scrolling and tap haptics independently.

### Is the full license a subscription?

No. It is one payment of $4.99 USD for a perpetual license, and future updates are included.

> One payment. A perpetual license, with future updates included.

### Does the app send my gestures or usage data anywhere?

No. Enabled interactions are processed locally on your Mac, and the developers do not receive usage analytics, interaction history or diagnostics from the app.

> We do not receive app-usage analytics, interaction history or diagnostics from the app.

### Will the trackpad haptics work on an Intel Mac?

No. Haptic and Material feedback need Apple silicon, so an Intel Mac only gets the click sound and the keyboard sound.

> Apple silicon for verified haptic and Material feedback; independent Click and Keyboard sound also support Intel

### Does it work with an external Magic Trackpad?

Yes, with the current USB-C Magic Trackpad. Other trackpads are checked by the app on your Mac, and the haptic controls only switch on if the device passes.

> Current USB-C Magic Trackpad; other trackpads are checked locally

### Will I feel haptics when I type on the keyboard?

No. Keyboard feedback is sound only, and that keyboard sound comes with the full license.

> keyboard feedback is audio only

## What it does not do

- It doesn't stay activated offline indefinitely beyond 30 days without periodic verification.

  > The full license is verified periodically and supports up to 30 days offline after a successful check.

- It doesn't provide audio or tactile previews for locked premium options in the demo.

  > Premium options stay locked and have no previews.

- It doesn't bundle the developer API Tester inside the main app installer.

  > It is a small, separate download. It is not bundled with FeelMyMac and does not replace the app or its license.

# Hoppvia

Launch your own branded taxi platform

- **Site:** [hoppvia.com](https://hoppvia.com/)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-03

Hoppvia is a white-label taxi and ride-hailing platform for businesses that want to launch under their own brand. It includes passenger and driver apps, dispatch and admin tools, live trip tracking, payments, fare controls, booking management and operational workflows in one platform.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Who is Hoppvia for?

Taxi fleet operators, private-hire operators, chauffeur companies, airport transfer brands and ride-hailing founders.

> Taxi fleet operators Private-hire operators Chauffeur companies Airport transfer brands Ride-hailing founders

### Which apps are included?

A customer app under your brand, a native driver app and admin and dispatch workflows for your team.

> Hoppvia is white-label taxi and ride-hailing software for transport businesses, with customer and driver apps, booking and dispatch workflows.

### Can the apps carry my own brand?

Yes. You add your name, logo, colors, service area, ride types and support details during brand setup.

> Add your name, logo, colors, service area, ride types, support details and customer copy.

### Does Hoppvia assist with submitting applications to app stores?

Yes, initial submission assistance for app stores is provided within the agreed launch scope.

> Initial app-store submission support is available within the agreed launch scope.

### Can transport operators review workflows before buying?

Yes, prospective clients can request a demo to review workflows, platform capabilities, and integration needs.

> Walk through the workflows, integrations and launch needs that matter to your team.

### Are third-party service fees included in the main quote?

No, third-party fees such as developer account costs, payment processing, or mapping usage charges are detailed separately.

> Your proposal identifies separate third-party usage charges, developer-account fees, applicable taxes or work outside the agreed scope.

### What does it cost per year?

From $1,990 a year, billed yearly in US dollars, with a $499 one-time setup fee.

> From $1,990 per year. Unlimited trips, 0% commission, hosting included.

### Do you take a cut of each trip?

No. There are no per-trip fees, no per-vehicle fees and no commission.

> No per-trip fees, no per-vehicle fees and 0% commission.

### How long until my apps are live in the stores?

Usually about a week. You test a real booking end to end first, then the apps go to the App Store and Google Play.

> Your apps then go to the App Store and Google Play, usually in about a week.

### Do the customer and driver apps work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Every plan includes branded customer and driver apps for iOS and Android.

> Branded customer app (iOS & Android)

### Can my existing bookings and data be moved over from my current system?

Only on the Enterprise plan. Data migration from your current system is listed as an Enterprise feature, and smaller fleets usually start fresh.

> Data migration from your current system

## What it does not do

- It doesn't publish fixed public pricing tiers.

  > Our current public pricing uses personalized quotations rather than standard fixed-price tiers.

- It doesn't offer a universal capacity guarantee for driver volume.

  > This is implementation experience, not a universal capacity guarantee; discuss your expected workload with our team.

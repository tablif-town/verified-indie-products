# GroupBlaster

Facebook Groups Auto Poster

- **Site:** [groupblaster.marbelona.com](https://groupblaster.marbelona.com/)
- **Topic:** [Marketing and sales](../topics/marketing-and-sales.md)

Post to your Facebook groups in minutes — no more tab-hopping. GroupBlaster is a Chrome extension for people who manage listings, announcements, or community updates across many Facebook Groups. Write your post once, pick the groups, and publish with full control over how much automation you allow — and on a paid plan, let a schedule dispatch it for you.

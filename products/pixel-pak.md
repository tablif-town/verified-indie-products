# Pixel Pak

The pixel art editor in your pocket. Free to start

- **Site:** [mipixel.co.uk/pixel-pak](https://mipixel.co.uk/pixel-pak)
- **Topic:** [Visual and media](../topics/visual-and-media.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-22

Pixel Pak is a pixel art editor built for touch, so drawing on a phone or tablet feels natural. It works on PC too. Layers and effects, animation, normal maps, hitboxes and game-ready exports. Swap a whole drawing to a retro palette like Game Boy or Master System, or to your own, with a before-and-after preview. Every layer and frame recolours at once, and one undo puts it back. Free to start, no account, and it works offline. Pro is a one-off £9.99.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Does it work with Unity?

Yes, with Pro. The zip bundle includes a Unity helper script that slices the sheet, sets point filtering, builds the animator and clips, and bakes hitboxes into your prefab in one click.

> Unity helper script in the bundle: one click slices the sheet, sets point filtering, builds the animator and clips, and bakes hitboxes into your prefab

### What can I export my art as?

PNG and GIF on the free version, and animations can go out as GIF, animated PNG or a sprite sheet. Pro adds the JSON atlas for sprite sheets, Tiled export and the zip bundle.

> Sprite-sheet atlas, Tiled export, zip bundle Not included Included

### Can I draw with my finger on a phone?

Yes. It was made for touch, and on a phone the layout rearranges so the tools, palette, layers and frames stay within reach. It works with a mouse on a PC too.

> On a phone it rearranges itself so everything is in easy reach.

### On how many devices can I use one Pro licence?

One licence works on up to 3 of your devices.

> One licence works on up to 3 of your devices.

### Can I open my Aseprite files?

Yes. You can open .aseprite, .ase and .pyxel files for free, and they open as a new drawing so your current work is safe.

> Open .aseprite, .ase and .pyxel files for free, as a new drawing so your open work is safe.

### How big can my canvas be in the free version?

The free version allows a 32 × 32 px canvas, 2 layers and 4 animation frames; Pro raises this to 512 × 512 px, 12 layers and 64 frames.

> Biggest canvas 32 × 32 px 512 × 512 px

### Do I need to download anything?

No. You can start free in your browser, and it installs like an app if you want it on your home screen.

> Start free in your browser, no sign-up, and unlock Pro for £9.99 when you need more.

### Does Pixel Pak work offline?

Yes. Install it once and it works without internet.

> Install once and it works without internet.

### Do I need an account?

No. The free version needs no sign-up.

> Free version, no sign-up

### Where are my drawings saved?

On your own device, as .pxpak project files.

> Projects save as .pxpak files on your own device.

### How many animation frames do I get for free?

Four frames in the free version, and up to 64 with Pro.

> Animation frames 4 64

### Is Pro a monthly subscription?

No. Pro is a single £9.99 payment and there is nothing to renew.

> Pro is one payment, no subscription

### If I upgrade later, will my free drawings still open?

Yes. Anything you made in the free version opens in Pro exactly as it was.

> Your free drawings open in Pro exactly as they are.

### Can I recolour a finished sprite into a Game Boy or PICO-8 palette?

Yes, and it is free. Every layer and frame switches at once, shading is kept, and one undo reverts it.

> Turn a whole drawing, every layer and frame, into Game Boy green, PICO-8, NES, Commodore 64, ZX Spectrum and more.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't let you save normal maps on the free version; you can only preview them.

  > Save normal maps (\_n.png) Not included Included

- It doesn't include hitboxes, 9-slice or the Unity helper on the free version.

  > Hitboxes, 9-slice and the Unity helper Not included Included

- It doesn't go past 4 animation frames on the free version (Pro allows 64).

  > Animation frames 4 64

- It doesn't include the lasso, blend, text, mirror or dither tools on the free version.

  > Lasso, Blend, Text, Mirror, Dither Not included

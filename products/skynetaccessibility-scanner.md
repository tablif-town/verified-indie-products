An automated website accessibility scanner and monitor enables organizations to proactively manage website accessibility through centralized testing, compliance tracking, and ongoing monitoring. An automated accessibility audit generates a detailed report, an accessibility score, and finding accessibility gaps. AI accessibility remediation recommendations help teams prioritize issues and streamline remediation. Built for development, QA, compliance, and digital teams, it provides visibility into accessibility risks, measures progress over time, and supports the delivery of more inclusive digital experiences. It aligns with major accessibility regulations and standards.

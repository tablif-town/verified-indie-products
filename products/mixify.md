# Mixify

Your music, your vibe — open source Android music player

- **Site:** [github.com/scisims12/Mixify](https://github.com/scisims12/Mixify)
- **Topic:** [Visual and media](../topics/visual-and-media.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-27

Meet Mixify, a free and open-source Android music player built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Enjoy smooth music streaming, smart synced lyrics, offline playback, personalized mixes, playlist management, and Listen Together with friends. Experience a beautiful Liquid Glass-inspired interface, background playback, and Google Cast support in compatible builds. Mixify is licensed under GPL-3.0. Discover music, connect with friends, and enjoy a fresh listening experience.

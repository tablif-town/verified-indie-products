### Does Cala HQ send weekly absence summaries to teams?

Cala HQ sends an automated email summary every Monday morning detailing who is scheduled to be away during the week.

> A clean Monday morning email showing everyone who is off this week.

### Is there a free tier for small teams?

Teams with up to 5 members can use Cala HQ completely free without providing a credit card.

> Free for up to 5 users · No credit card required

### Can managers enforce minimum notice for leave requests?

Managers can set a required minimum notice period in days to prevent last-minute leave submissions.

> Prevent last-minute surprises by requiring a set number of days' notice for requests.

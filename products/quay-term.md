# Quay Term

Your SSH hosts, terminals and file transfers in one window.

- **Site:** [quayterm.com](https://quayterm.com/)
- **Topic:** [Developer](../topics/developer.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-13

A free desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux. Save sessions, split panes, forward ports and move files, with prod and staging labels so you can tell your servers apart.

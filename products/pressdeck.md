# PressDeck

Turn Android volume buttons into useful shortcuts

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.pressdeck](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.pressdeck)
- **Topic:** [Desktop and utilities](../topics/desktop-and-utilities.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-21

PressDeck turns supported Volume Up and Volume Down double presses and long presses into shortcuts for actions you choose, including launching apps, controlling media, opening Android settings, toggling the flashlight, or asking Android to take a system screenshot. Unlike a general macro engine, it stays focused and deterministic. A guided compatibility test checks what your device can support, and accepted button events are processed on-device.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Can I remap the AI or Bixby button on my phone?

On some phones, yes. When the button is exposed in a way PressDeck can detect, it can verify it and offer a single-press mapping.

> When supported, PressDeck can verify the button on your device and offer a single-press mapping.

### What does the Pro upgrade add?

The optional lifetime Pro upgrade unlocks more active shortcuts, Next and Previous track, and selected Android settings shortcuts.

> The optional lifetime Pro upgrade unlocks additional eligible active shortcuts plus Next track, Previous track, and selected Android settings shortcuts.

### Can PressDeck remap the power button?

No. It does not remap the power button and does not claim support for every hardware button.

> PressDeck does not remap the power button

### Can I chain several actions on one press?

No. PressDeck is made for simple, predictable shortcuts with one gesture and one action, not macros or multi-step automation.

> PressDeck is built for simple, predictable hardware button shortcuts rather than complex automation or macro workflows.

### Do I need a PressDeck account?

No. Button remapping and shortcut setup run on your phone, and there is no PressDeck cloud service behind them.

> PressDeck’s core button remapping and shortcut configuration run on your device.

### Do the shortcuts work when the screen is off?

It depends on the phone. Screen state is one of the things the test checks, and some shortcuts may not be available in every state.

> Some volume button shortcuts or device-button mappings may not be available on every phone or in every state.

### Will it work on my phone?

It depends on the phone. The guided compatibility test checks what your phone actually delivers, and PressDeck reports what passed instead of promising every gesture works.

> The app tests what your phone actually delivers, including supported volume button gestures, screen-state behavior, and eligible dedicated device buttons.

### Can PressDeck read my screen or what I type?

No. It does not use the service to read screen content or typed text, to tap or swipe for you, or to capture screenshot pixels.

> PressDeck does not use AccessibilityService to read screen content, read typed text

### Why does PressDeck need Accessibility access?

To filter hardware key presses. That is how it spots supported volume gestures and certain tested device buttons.

> PressDeck uses Android AccessibilityService for physical hardware-key filtering

### Can a volume button open a specific app?

Yes. Launching an installed app is one of the actions you can put on a double press or long press.

> Launch an installed app

### How many shortcuts can I use for free?

One. The free version allows one active shortcut, and it can run Play/Pause, an app launch, the camera, the flashlight or a system screenshot.

> Free includes one active shortcut plus actions such as Play / Pause, app launch, camera launch, flashlight toggle, and Android’s screenshot action.

### Can a button skip to the next song?

Only with Pro. Next track and Previous track shortcuts are part of the lifetime Pro upgrade, while Play / Pause is free.

> Skip to the next or previous media track with Pro

### Does PressDeck see my screenshots?

No. It asks Android to take the screenshot and never receives, inspects or stores the pixels.

> does not receive, inspect, store, or process the screenshot pixels

### Are there ads in PressDeck?

No. It does not use ads, an analytics SDK or a PressDeck cloud service.

> PressDeck does not require a PressDeck account, ads, an analytics SDK, or a PressDeck cloud service.

### Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase handled through Google Play.

> Pro is a one-time purchase handled through Google Play.

### Can a button open a specific Android settings screen?

Yes, with Pro. Selected Android settings shortcuts are part of the Pro upgrade.

> Next track, Previous track, and selected Android settings shortcuts.

### Will shortcuts work with Do Not Disturb on?

It depends on the phone. Button behavior can change with Do Not Disturb, the audio route and power management.

> Do Not Disturb settings, active audio route, and power management.

### Which presses can I assign?

Supported double-press and long-press gestures on Volume Up and Volume Down, plus a single press on some dedicated device buttons.

> Assign supported double-press and long-press gestures to actions

### Are my button presses sent anywhere?

No. Accepted button events are processed on the phone and are not sent or shared.

> Accepted button events are processed on-device and are not sent or shared.

### Is PressDeck free to download?

Yes. It is free on Google Play, and the free version includes one active shortcut.

> Free includes one active shortcut plus actions such as Play / Pause, app launch, camera launch, flashlight toggle, and Android’s screenshot action.

### Can a button open the camera?

Yes. Opening the camera is one of the actions you can put on a supported volume button gesture, and it is included in the free version.

> Open the camera

### Does play and pause work with every media app?

It works with compatible media. Some players or audio states may not respond the same way on every phone.

> Play or pause compatible media

### Does PressDeck use an analytics SDK?

No. It does not need an account, ads, an analytics SDK or a cloud service of its own.

> PressDeck does not require a PressDeck account, ads, an analytics SDK, or a PressDeck cloud service.

### What happens to other keys the service can see?

PressDeck ignores them. It only acts on keys in its supported allowlist.

> PressDeck ignores keys outside its supported allowlist.

### Can PressDeck tap, swipe or control my phone remotely?

No. It does not tap or swipe the interface, take autonomous actions or control the device remotely.

> tap or swipe the interface, perform autonomous UI actions, remotely control your device

### Is PressDeck meant as a disability assistance tool?

No. It is a button remapping utility, even though it uses the Accessibility service to filter hardware keys.

> PressDeck is a button remapping utility and is not an accessibility tool for disability assistance.

### Who makes PressDeck?

The developer listed on Google Play is Quazmoz.

> Developer: Quazmoz

### Is there an iPhone version of PressDeck?

No. PressDeck is an Android app for supported Android phones.

> Turn your phone’s physical buttons into useful shortcuts with PressDeck, a focused Android button remapper and volume button remapper for supported devices.

### How does the compatibility test work?

It checks what your own phone actually sends when you press the buttons. That covers volume gestures, how they behave in different screen states, and any eligible dedicated buttons.

> The app tests what your phone actually delivers, including supported volume button gestures, screen-state behavior, and eligible dedicated device buttons.

### What happens if a gesture isn't supported on my phone?

PressDeck won't present it as a feature you can count on. The test is there so you only set up gestures your device really delivers.

> PressDeck includes guided compatibility testing so unsupported gestures are not presented as guaranteed features.

### Why might a shortcut work on one phone but not on another?

Android makers handle physical buttons differently. Android version, software build, screen state and power management can all change what the app receives.

> Physical button behavior can vary by Android version, phone manufacturer, software build, screen state, media state, Do Not Disturb settings, active audio route, and power management.

### Can headphones or media playback change how the buttons behave?

They can. The active audio route and media state are both listed as things that affect button behavior.

> Physical button behavior can vary by Android version, phone manufacturer, software build, screen state, media state, Do Not Disturb settings, active audio route, and power management.

### Does the flashlight shortcut work on every phone?

Only on supported devices. Run the compatibility test and you'll see what your phone can do.

> Toggle the flashlight on supported devices

### Does PressDeck work offline?

Yes, for the core features. Button remapping and shortcut setup run on your phone, with no PressDeck cloud service.

> PressDeck’s core button remapping and shortcut configuration run on your device.

### How do I pay for Pro?

Through Google Play, as a one-time purchase.

> Pro is a one-time purchase handled through Google Play.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't remap the power button.

  > PressDeck does not remap the power button, claim support for every hardware button, or assume compatibility from the device model alone.

- It doesn't run macros or multi-step automations.

  > PressDeck is built for simple, predictable hardware button shortcuts rather than complex automation or macro workflows.

- It isn't an accessibility tool for disability assistance, even though it uses the AccessibilityService.

  > PressDeck is a button remapping utility and is not an accessibility tool for disability assistance.

- It doesn't guarantee that every gesture or device button works on every phone.

  > Some volume button shortcuts or device-button mappings may not be available on every phone or in every state.

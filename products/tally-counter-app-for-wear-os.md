# Tally Counter App for Wear OS

Count laps, reps, people and more from your Wear OS watch

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.simplecounter](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.simplecounter)
- **Topic:** [Desktop and utilities](../topics/desktop-and-utilities.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-21

Tally Counter is a standalone Wear OS counter for laps, reps, inventory, attendance, scores and more. Keep up to eight named counters, use rotary input, set targets, and access the active tally from a Tile or watch-face complication.

### Does every tap vibrate, or only some?

Every increment gives a light haptic. Crossing a target gives one stronger buzz, so the two feel different on your wrist.

### If I move to a new watch, do my counts come with me?

No. Counts live only on the watch they were made on, and the founder says app backups are disabled, so nothing carries over.

### Do I still get the target buzz if a big step jumps past the target?

### What is the difference between resetting one counter group and resetting all?

## What it does not do

- It doesn't count anything automatically. Every count is a tap or a turn of the crown or bezel.

  > any other manual tally

- It doesn't hold more than eight counters at a time.

  > Create and save up to eight counters

- It doesn't allow a step size above 100.

  > Choose step sizes from 1 to 100

- It doesn't undo older resets, only the most recent one.

  > Confirm resets and undo the most recent reset

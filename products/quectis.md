# Quectis

Make consequential decisions with evidence, not instinct.

- **Site:** [quectis.vecorum.com](https://quectis.vecorum.com/)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-16

Quectis is an AI decision-support workspace for founders and product leaders facing consequential choices. It generates competing strategies, tests assumptions against available evidence, recommends a path with transparent scores and confidence limits, then turns the decision into a bounded execution plan.

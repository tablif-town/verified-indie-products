# Bretzel SEO

All-in-one local SEO toolkit for agencies and freelancers

- **Site:** [bretzel-seo.fr](https://bretzel-seo.fr/)
- **Topic:** [Startup](../topics/startup.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-23

Most SEO tools are built for big content sites. Bretzel SEO is built for local. Run the local audit, crawl the site, check NAP citations across directories, analyze the Google Business Profile, track keywords and pull PageSpeed, all in one place. Manage your clients by organization, then export a white-label PDF report under your own branding. The AI assistant turns findings into a step-by-step action plan your client can actually follow. Free plan, no credit card.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Does Bretzel SEO check my business listings in directories?

Yes. It checks local citations, meaning how consistent your business details are across directories.

> Vérifier les citations locales

### How much do the paid plans cost?

Billed yearly, Starter is 24,17 € per month (290 € a year), Pro is 49,17 € per month (590 € a year) and Agency is 107,50 € per month (1 290 € a year).

> Facturé 590 € par an

### Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan lets you try the local SEO basics without a commitment.

> Pour découvrir les essentiels du SEO local, sans engagement.

### Does it work with Claude Code or Cursor?

Yes, through an MCP server. The pricing table lists it for Claude Code, Cursor and similar tools, and it comes with the higher plans.

> Serveur MCP (Claude Code, Cursor…)

### Can the reports carry my agency's branding?

Yes. Each report can follow your own visual identity or your client's, so you can send it as your own work.

> Adaptez chaque rapport à votre identité ou à celle de votre client, prêt à livrer et prêt à convaincre.

### Can I audit a single page instead of a whole site?

Yes. You can check one specific page or a set of URLs, depending on what the client needs.

> Évaluez une page précise ou un ensemble d’URLs selon vos besoins clients.

### How many pages can it crawl each month?

50 pages on Free, 1,000 on Starter, 5,000 on Pro and 25,000 on Agency.

> Pages crawlées / mois 50 1 000 5 000 25 000

### Can several people on my team use one account?

Only on Agency, which allows 5 users. Free, Starter and Pro are for 1 user each.

> Utilisateurs 1 1 1 5

### How detailed is the Google Maps visibility grid?

It depends on the plan. Starter checks a 5 × 5 grid of 25 points, Pro goes up to 9 × 9 (81 points) and Agency up to 11 × 11 (121 points).

> Précision de la grille de visibilité 5 × 5 (25 points) Jusqu'à 9 × 9 (81 points) Jusqu'à 11 × 11 (121 points)

### Is there a minimum commitment?

No. Every plan can be cancelled at any time, and paying yearly instead of monthly gives you two months free.

> Sans engagement. Résiliable à tout moment.

### Do I need a credit card to try it?

No. You can run a first analysis without entering a card, and the home page says the result comes back in about 30 seconds.

> Aucune carte bancaire nécessaire · résultat en 30 secondes

### Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can move up or down at any time from your account, the change applies immediately and billing is adjusted pro rata.

> Oui, vous pouvez passer à une formule supérieure ou inférieure à tout moment depuis votre espace.

### Is there a discount for paying yearly?

Yes. Annual billing gives two months free compared with monthly.

> 2 mois offerts

### How are payments handled?

Through Stripe, whose infrastructure follows the PCI-DSS card security standard.

> Les paiements sont traités par Stripe

### Does it track local keyword rankings?

Yes. Local keyword tracking is one of the modules, and the number of tracked keywords grows with the plan.

> Suivi des mots-clés locaux

### Can I connect Search Console and Google Analytics?

Yes. Search Console and Google Analytics, including traffic from AI assistants, are listed among the plan features.

> Search Console et Google Analytics (trafic, assistants IA)

### Does it check page speed and Core Web Vitals?

Yes. A performance module covers Core Web Vitals and speed issues that affect user experience.

> Core Web Vitals & performance

### Does it replace an SEO agency?

No. It is meant to help an agency structure and present its expertise, not to replace it.

> Bretzel SEO ne remplace pas l’expertise d’une agence de référencement naturel.

### Where does Bretzel SEO come from?

It is a local SEO audit SaaS made in Alsace, France.

> Bretzel SEO est un SaaS d’audit SEO local, né en Alsace.

### How many keywords can I track on each plan?

It depends on the plan. Free tracks 10 keywords, Starter 100, Pro 400 and Agency 1,500.

> Mots-clés suivis

### Does the free plan include the Google Maps visibility grid?

No. The pricing table lists visibility zone checks from Starter (10 a month), with 40 on Pro and unlimited on Agency.

> Relevés de zone de visibilité / mois

### How many Google Business Profile checks do I get a month?

Free gives you 2 Google Business Profile and citation analyses a month, Starter 20, Pro 100 and Agency has no cap.

> Analyses GBP + citations / mois

### Can I keep separate organizations for different teams or brands?

Yes, within limits. Free and Starter allow 1 organization, Pro 3 and Agency 10.

> Organisations

### Is white label included in every plan?

Not clearly. The pricing table shows white label as an add-on, so check with the team which plan covers it before you promise branded reports to a client.

> Marque blanche

### What format is the client report in?

A full PDF report with a visual summary and key indicators. You can send it directly or export it to share.

> Rapport complet au format PDF

### Does it tell me which fixes to do first?

Yes. Recommendations are sorted by priority, grouped by SEO theme and given an estimate of potential impact.

> Tri des recommandations par priorité

### What is the audit based on?

Google's own local ranking criteria. The team says the audit follows the official guidance in the Google Business Profile help center.

> Notre audit s’appuie sur les critères officiels de positionnement local détaillés dans le centre d’aide Google Business Profile.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't replace the expertise of an SEO agency. It helps you structure and present that expertise.

  > Bretzel SEO ne remplace pas l’expertise d’une agence de référencement naturel.

- It doesn't allow more than one client project on the free plan.

  > Clients / projets 1 Illimité Illimité Illimité

- It doesn't give more than one user seat below the Agency plan.

  > Utilisateurs 1 1 1 5

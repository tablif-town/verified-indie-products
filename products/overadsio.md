# overads.io

Automate marketing across ads and social, then approve actions

- **Site:** [overads.io](https://overads.io/)
- **Topic:** [Marketing and sales](../topics/marketing-and-sales.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-16

overads unifies marketing automation, content creation, publishing, ad analytics, and AI search visibility in one workspace. It monitors your ad accounts, organic posts, and AI responses to your brand. It drafts actions like pausing costly campaigns, writing reports, and generating posts for your approval. Connect major ad and social platforms, run plain-English workflows on schedules or triggers, and manage everything from a single dashboard without adding seats or extra tools.

# FetchAhead

Save pages, files and feeds offline before you lose signal

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.fetchahead](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.fetchahead)
- **Topic:** [Work and office](../topics/work-and-office.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-21

FetchAhead groups pages, files, PDFs, feeds and supported video files into Offline Packs for a trip, commute or study session, prepared while you still have signal. Share links in from any Android app, and Smart Scope can pull in nearby pages or a docs section. It shows what is really stored offline versus what may be out of date, and a failed refresh keeps the last good copy. Local-first, no account. JavaScript-heavy or logged-in sites may only save a limited version.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Is everything free?

The app is free, and some scale and automation features come with optional Pro access.

> Some scale and automation features are available through optional Pro access.

### Can I search what I saved?

Yes. Saved material opens in the offline Reader, and you can search across supported captured content.

> search across supported captured content

### Can I add links from other apps?

Yes. You can share supported links, text and files into FetchAhead from other Android apps, or add content from inside the app.

> Share supported links, text and files directly into FetchAhead or add content from inside the app.

### Will automatic refresh use up my battery or storage?

It follows your settings. Content you choose refreshes only when the connection, storage and battery settings you set allow it.

> Choose content to refresh automatically when your connection, storage and battery settings allow.

### Can I save articles behind a paywall or a login?

Only what you can already see, and maybe not all of it. FetchAhead does not bypass paywalls, logins or other access controls.

> does not bypass DRM, paywalls, authentication or access controls

### Will every website work offline?

No. Some JavaScript-heavy, logged-in or streaming sites can't be fully captured and may save as a limited version instead.

> Some modern JavaScript-heavy, authenticated or streaming sites may not be fully capturable and can fall back to a limited representation.

### Which video files does it save?

Supported public progressive MP4 and WebM files up to 64 MiB, saved through its direct video action.

> FetchAhead can save supported public progressive MP4 and WebM files up to 64 MiB through its direct video action.

### Can it download YouTube or other streaming videos?

No. It is not a streaming-site downloader and it does not get around DRM.

> It is not a streaming-site downloader and does not bypass DRM, paywalls, authentication or access controls.

### What can I save into a pack?

Web pages, files, PDFs, images, feeds and supported public video files. You can mix them in the same pack.

> Add pages, files, PDFs, images, feeds and supported public video files to packs you can return to later.

### Do I need an account or a cloud service?

No. Core use works without a FetchAhead account or cloud service, and your saved library stays on the device.

> Core use does not require a FetchAhead account or cloud service.

### What happens when a saved page gets old?

It stays readable. FetchAhead tracks freshness separately, so a stale copy is marked as possibly out of date instead of becoming unusable.

> keeps freshness separate from availability so an older saved copy does not suddenly become unusable just because it is stale

### What is Smart Scope?

A way to grow a capture from one page. It can add nearby pages, a relevant section, a documentation structure or a feed, while you keep control of how far it goes.

> Start with one page and expand the capture when you need more context.

### What is the Reader view?

A clean, simplified version of a captured web page for reading offline. Some content is saved in another supported form instead.

> Captured web content can be normalized into a clean Reader view

### Can I make separate packs for different plans?

Yes. You can build a pack for a trip, a commute, a study session or a work project and keep each one apart.

> Build a pack for a trip, commute, study session, work project

### How is a pack different from bookmarks?

A pack groups the content itself by situation, stored on the phone, instead of a list of links that may not open later.

> Group saved content by situation instead of collecting disconnected bookmarks.

### If a refresh fails, do I lose the saved copy?

No. A failed refresh keeps the previous good local copy instead of replacing it with a broken one.

> Failed refreshes keep the previous good local copy instead of replacing it with a broken one.

### Can I delete things I no longer need?

Yes. You can delete items one by one or clear the whole library in the app.

> can be deleted item by item or cleared from the app

### What does Pro add?

Some scale and automation features. The core saving and reading work without Pro.

> Some scale and automation features are available through optional Pro access.

### Is there an iPhone version?

Not in the sources. FetchAhead is an Android app on Google Play.

> FetchAhead: Offline Cache - Google Play

## What it does not do

- It doesn't download from streaming sites or get around DRM, paywalls or logins.

  > It is not a streaming-site downloader and does not bypass DRM, paywalls, authentication or access controls.

- It doesn't always capture JavaScript-heavy or logged-in sites in full; some save only a limited version.

  > Some modern JavaScript-heavy, authenticated or streaming sites may not be fully capturable and can fall back to a limited representation.

- It doesn't save video files larger than 64 MiB or formats other than progressive MP4 and WebM.

  > FetchAhead can save supported public progressive MP4 and WebM files up to 64 MiB through its direct video action.

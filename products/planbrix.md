# PlanBrix

Run your creator business, all in one place.

- **Site:** [planbrix.online](https://www.planbrix.online/)
- **Topic:** [Work and office](../topics/work-and-office.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-12

PlanBrix is an all-in-one business workspace for creators and freelancers. Manage clients, projects, rate cards, brand deals, quotes, invoices, payments and reminders from one place. With built-in tools and an AI assistant to help you stay organized, PlanBrix takes care of the business side so you can focus on your creative work. Start free.

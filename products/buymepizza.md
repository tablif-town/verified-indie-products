# BuyMePizza

Accept crypto tips with 0% fees. 100% direct to your wallet

- **Site:** [buymepizza.com](https://buymepizza.com/)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-23

Stop losing 5–12% of your creator earnings to platform cuts and payout delays. BuyMePizza is the ultimate zero-fee crypto alternative to Buy Me a Coffee and Patreon. Accept instant, direct-to-wallet crypto tips and donations across 10+ top blockchains, including SOL, ETH, BTC, USDT, and DOGE, with zero platform fees and zero chargeback risk. Built for developers, YouTubers, and Web3 creators alike. Claim your custom handle, list your wallet addresses in 60 seconds, and start earning today!

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Which coins can fans tip in?

SOL, ETH, BTC, USDT, USDC, BNB, DOGE, ADA, XRP, AVAX, POL and LTC, sent directly to the wallet addresses you add.

> Fans send SOL, ETH, BTC, USDT, USDC, BNB, DOGE, ADA, XRP, AVAX, POL & LTC directly to your wallet addresses.

### How much is one pizza?

A pizza is $5, and supporters can pick 1, 3 or 5 pizzas.

> Select Pizzas ($5 / pizza)

### Where can I share my tipping page?

You can put the link in a YouTube bio, a TikTok link or a Twitch overlay, and the site lists GitHub, Substack, Discord, WordPress and Webflow among compatible channels.

> Share your page link in your YouTube bio, TikTok link, or Twitch overlay.

### Does BuyMePizza take a cut of each tip?

No. The platform fee is 0%. The demo on the home page shows a $15 tip landing as the full $15, paid in SOL.

> Direct Wallet Payout: 100% ($15 USD in SOL)

### Do I have to share my private keys or seed phrase?

No. You only paste your public receiving address, the same kind of thing you give someone so they can pay you. The site says it will never ask for seed phrases, private keys or passwords.

> We will never ask for seed phrases, private keys, or passwords.

### Does BuyMePizza hold the money before it reaches me?

No. A tip moves from the fan's wallet to yours on the blockchain. BuyMePizza never has custody, so it can't freeze the money or sit on a payout.

> We hold zero custody and cannot freeze your funds or delay payouts.

### Do I need a bank account or ID to start?

No. You don't connect a bank, share a home address or upload a government ID to receive tips. A wallet address is enough.

> You never have to connect a bank account, expose your address, or upload government ID to start receiving crypto donations.

### Can a supporter reverse a tip later?

No. A confirmed blockchain transfer can't be disputed the way a card or PayPal payment can, so there are no chargebacks months later. It also means a tip sent by mistake can't be pulled back through BuyMePizza.

> blockchain transactions are mathematically permanent and irreversible

### Which networks can USDT and USDC tips arrive on?

The coin list shows USDT on ERC20, TRC20 and Solana, and USDC on ERC20, Solana and Polygon. Check that each address you paste belongs to the network it is listed under.

> Tether USD ERC20 / TRC20 / Solana

### Can I pick my own page address?

Yes. You claim a handle in the form buymepizza.com/yourname, then add an avatar, a bio and your social links. The site puts that setup at under 60 seconds.

> Choose your custom handle (\`buymepizza.com/yourname\`). Set up your avatar, bio, and social links in under 60 seconds.

### What can I put on the page besides wallet addresses?

A bio, photos and custom tips. That gives fans a reason to stay on the page for a moment instead of seeing a bare list of addresses.

> Create your free crypto tips page with a bio, photos, and custom tips.

### How fast do tips arrive?

It depends on the coin. Solana tips arrive in about a second, Ethereum in about 15 seconds and Bitcoin in 10 to 30 minutes.

> Solana tips arrive in \~1 second, Ethereum in \~15 seconds, and Bitcoin in 10–30 minutes directly into your personal wallet.

### Can I set my own price per pizza?

Yes. You choose the pizza price when you set up your page, for example $5 per pizza.

> set your custom pizza price (e.g. $5 USD per pizza slice)

### How long does setup take?

About a minute. You claim a handle, add your bio and paste your wallet addresses.

> Takes 60 seconds

### Does it cost anything to create a page?

No. Creating a page is free to start, and there is no platform fee on tips.

> Free to start

### Can fans tip by scanning a code?

Yes. Fans can tip with one click or by scanning.

> Fans tip with 1 click or scan, and crypto transfers directly to your wallet.

### Can I choose how much one pizza costs?

Yes. You set your own pizza price when you set up the page, for example $5 per pizza.

> set your custom pizza price (e.g. $5 USD per pizza slice)

### Can BuyMePizza freeze or hold back my tips?

No. Tips go from the fan's wallet straight to yours, and the platform holds no custody, so it cannot freeze funds or delay payouts.

> We hold zero custody and cannot freeze your funds or delay payouts.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't process card, PayPal or bank payments. Every tip is a crypto transfer from the fan's wallet.

  > Tips go directly from the fan's wallet straight into your self-custody wallet on the blockchain.

- It doesn't accept coins outside its list of 12.

  > Select Crypto Coin (12 Coins Available)

- It doesn't convert tips into your local currency. What arrives is the coin the fan picked.

  > Fans choose their favorite coin.

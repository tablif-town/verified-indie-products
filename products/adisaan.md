# AdiSaan

The arena where people and AI answer the same question

- **Site:** [adisaan.com](https://adisaan.com/)
- **Topic:** [Marketing and sales](../topics/marketing-and-sales.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-18

People answer in their own words. The models answer through a public endpoint and appear beside them under their own names — Claude, Gemini — never as a human voice. You choose whether yours stays private. With consent, the answers become a study of how people imagine the future. The models agree with each other. People don't. Ten questions, one open at a time. A living map shows where each answer came from. Free, in eleven languages.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Can users choose to keep their submitted answers private?

Yes, individuals select their privacy preference when submitting a response to determine if it remains private.

> You choose whether yours stays private.

### When does Muse generate and update answer insights?

Muse synthesis begins once at least 10 public responses are gathered, updating after every 10 additional public answers.

> Muse starts after at least 10 public answers and updates after every 10 more.

### Is there any fee to submit responses or read public answers?

No, submitting responses and reading all public answers on the platform is entirely free of charge.

> Answering stays free, and so does reading every public answer.

### Which languages can I use the site in?

Eleven. English, Romanian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech and Hungarian.

> English, Romanian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech and Hungarian.

### Which AI models answer the questions?

Models such as Claude and Gemini. They answer through a public endpoint and are always shown under their own names, never as a person.

> The models answer through a public endpoint and appear beside them under their own names

### Will the site ask for my card or bank details?

No. The site never asks for card or bank details. Donations go by bank transfer to the partner NGO's account or through the official tax form.

> This site never asks for your card or bank details.

### What is the question open right now?

Question 01 asks how you would imagine making one million euro, in your own words.

> What is the best way you imagine to make one million euro?

### Does the site track me with analytics cookies?

Only if you allow it. Google Analytics cookies are set only after you click "Allow analytics", and ad personalisation and Google signals stay off.

> Analytics cookies are used only after "Allow analytics"

### Does a donation give me extra access or rewards?

No. A donation buys no reward, no access and no share, and the money goes to the partner NGO Asociația Inotech for hardware.

> A donation buys no reward, no access and no share in anything.

### Are answers processed on the project's own hardware?

Not yet. AdiSaan and Muse run on rented AI today, and a GPU server and an offline Mac are fundraising goals.

> AdiSaan and Muse run on rented AI today.

### Can I listen to the results instead of reading them?

Yes. Question 01 has an episode of about an hour, made with NotebookLM and split into chapters, that you can play in the browser or download.

> POWERED BY NOTEBOOKLM

## What it does not do

- It doesn't allow more than one open question at a time.

  > Ten questions, one open at a time.

- It doesn't collect hardware donations directly through Adrian or the AdiSaan entity.

  > Donations go to our partner NGO, Asociația Inotech · Association pour l’Économie Circulaire (AEC), not to Adrian personally and not to AdiSaan or AIOHM.

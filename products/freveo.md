# Freveo

A freelancer workspace that won’t let you start unpaid work

- **Site:** [freveo.app](https://freveo.app/)
- **Topic:** [Startup](../topics/startup.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-06

Clients, projects and invoices in one account, with two rules: no project starts until the deposit is paid, and your clients write reviews you can't hide. Plus contracts from a form, a client portal with chat and a public page for your work. Free plan, no card.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Is time tracking included on the Pro plan?

No. Time tracking with hourly billing is only on Max, which costs $15.99 a month.

> Everything in Pro, plus unlimited chat with files, unlimited portal clients, time tracking with hourly billing, analytics

### Can I add a teammate to my account?

No. Freveo is billed per account, and there are no extra seats to add.

> Freveo is billed per account. There is nobody to add, no seats to count

### Is a credit card required to sign up for the free plan?

No, you can sign up and use the free plan without entering payment details. A card is only requested if you decide to upgrade to a paid plan or purchase extra credit packs.

> No. Create an account and work on the free plan. A card is only asked for when you subscribe or buy a credit pack.

### Do unused monthly AI credits roll over to the following month?

No, your monthly allowance resets at the start of each month in UTC time and does not carry over. However, credits purchased from add-on packs never expire.

> Your monthly allowance doesn't. It starts again at the beginning of each month, UTC.

### How many client portal invites are included on the Pro plan?

The Pro plan allows you to send up to 10 client portal invites. If you need unlimited client portal invites, you must upgrade to the Max plan.

> Up to 10 client portal invites

### Does it mark invoices overdue for me?

Yes. Invoices are numbered and emailed for you, then marked overdue the day after they are due.

> Numbered for you, sent by email, and marked overdue the day after it's due.

### Can I see when a client opens an invoice?

Yes. The invoice goes out by email with the PDF attached, and you see when it arrives and when it is opened.

> You see when it lands and when they open it.

### Can I write contracts in it?

Yes. NDAs, service agreements and statements of work are filled in from a form and sent as a PDF.

> NDAs, service agreements and statements of work, filled in from a form and sent as a PDF.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't process or hold money transferred between freelancers and their clients.

  > They pay you directly; Freveo never handles that money.

- It doesn't roll over unused monthly AI credit allowances to subsequent billing cycles.

  > Your monthly allowance doesn't. It starts again at the beginning of each month, UTC.

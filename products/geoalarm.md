# GeoAlarm

Fell asleep on the bus? Wake up at your stop

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.relacosm.geospatialalarm](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.relacosm.geospatialalarm)
- **Topic:** [Data](../topics/data.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-24

GeoAlarm is a free Android alarm that rings based on where you are, not what time it is. Pick your stop on the map, lock your phone and doze off: it rings when you get close, even with the screen off and the app closed. Motion alarms ring if someone picks up your phone or bag, and altitude alarms use the barometer to ring at a set height. No account, no ads, 30 languages. Your alarms and location stay on your phone.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Will GeoAlarm ring if my phone is set to silent mode?

Yes, GeoAlarm overrides silent settings to sound alerts at full alarm volume.

> Rings at alarm volume, even when your phone is on silent

### Does GeoAlarm require an internet connection to trigger location alarms?

No, an internet connection is only needed when searching addresses and loading map data. Once you set a location alarm, it will ring without internet access.

> Place alarms need internet only to load the map and search; once set, they ring without internet

### Does GeoAlarm send user location data to external servers?

No, all alarms and location information remain stored locally on your device. GeoAlarm does not transmit your personal location data.

> GeoAlarm never sends your location or alarms to us.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't display altitude alarms on mobile devices that lack a built-in barometer.

  > Only shown on phones that have a barometer.

- It doesn't run reliably in the background on specific phone brands unless battery optimization settings are modified.

  > Some phone makers (Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Realme, Vivo, OnePlus) stop background apps to save battery.

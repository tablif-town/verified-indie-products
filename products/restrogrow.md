# RestroGrow

AI-Powered Restaurant Management Software

- **Site:** [restrogrow.com](https://restrogrow.com/)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-19

RestroGrow is AI-powered restaurant management software for restaurants, cafes and cloud kitchens. Get your own 0% commission online ordering website, POS & inventory, WhatsApp marketing, a loyalty program, QR menu ordering and AI-assisted Google review replies, all in one dashboard. Bring your delivery app regulars back to your own channel and own your customer data.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Which printers work with RestroGrow?

Thermal printers. Bills and kitchen tickets print over Bluetooth, USB or the network.

> Prints on thermal printers over Bluetooth, USB or the network

### Can I split one bill across different payment methods?

Yes. One bill can be split across cash, UPI and card, or collected later.

> Split one bill across cash, UPI and card, or collect later

### Is the payment gateway fee included in the monthly price?

No. If you take online payments on your website, the gateway's fee is charged separately.

> If you take online payments, the payment gateway's fee is separate.

### Does Restro Grow work without internet?

No. It is cloud-based and needs internet; an offline mode for basic order taking is in progress, so a backup connection like a mobile hotspot is recommended.

> Restro Grow is cloud-based, so you need internet to use it.

### Can it connect to my accounting or inventory software?

Not yet. It currently works as a standalone system, and integrations with accounting, inventory and delivery platforms are being worked on.

> Currently, Restro Grow works as a standalone system.

### Can I manage more than one restaurant?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan you can manage multiple locations from one dashboard, each with its own menu, staff and settings.

> Yes, with our Enterprise plan. You can manage multiple locations from one dashboard

### Is there a discount for paying yearly?

Yes. You can pay monthly or annually, and annual billing saves you two months.

> You can pay monthly or annually (save 2 months with annual billing).

### Can I cancel and still take my data with me?

Yes. You can cancel anytime from account settings, your plan runs to the end of the billing period, and you can export all your data.

> After that, you'll lose access but can export all your data.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't share payments or customer details through its API.

  > Payments and customer details are not part of the API.

- It doesn't cover more than 5 tables on the Small Restaurant plan.

  > For 1–5 tables

- It doesn't allow more than 20 working API keys per restaurant.

  > A restaurant can have up to 20 working keys.

# ToolsGrid

Free browser tools for PDFs, images, file compression, resumes and QR codes.

- **Site:** [toolsgrid.online](https://toolsgrid.online/)
- **Topic:** [Work and office](../topics/work-and-office.md)

Free browser tools to edit PDFs, convert images, compress files, create resumes, and generate QR codes privately. No account or sign-up required.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Can I sign a document with ToolsGrid?

Yes. You can draw or type a signature on the signature pad and export it as a transparent PNG to place on your documents.

> Draw or type signatures on an online digital signature pad with auto-cropped transparent PNG export.

### Can I come back later and keep editing my resume?

Yes. Resume drafts are saved in your browser's local storage, so you can close the page and continue later on the same device.

> All draft changes persist in private client localStorage

### Can I make a QR code for my Wi-Fi network?

Yes. The QR code generator works with website links, plain text and Wi-Fi networks, and exports PNG or SVG.

> Generate high-resolution QR codes for website URLs, plain text, and Wi-Fi networks in crisp PNG or SVG.

### Do my PDFs get uploaded when I merge or split them?

No. The founder confirms all the PDF tools run in your browser without uploading the file.

> Q: Do all the PDF tools run fully in the browser, without uploading the file? A (founder): Yes

### Is ToolsGrid really free, or is there a catch at download?

It is free. There are no watermarks, no download fees and no trial that runs out.

> No bait-and-switch watermarks, hidden download fees, or trials that expire after three days.

### How large a PDF can the merge tool handle?

Roughly 50 to 100 MB, or about 100 to 300 pages, before the browser starts to slow down.

> Up to 50 MB – 100 MB (\~100 to 300 pages)

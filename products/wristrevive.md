# WristRevive

Test why your Wear OS watch battery drains so fast

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.wristrevive](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.wristrevive)
- **Topic:** [Desktop and utilities](../topics/desktop-and-utilities.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-21

WristRevive runs 12 or 24-hour Revive Scans and one-hour Idle Drain Tests on your Wear OS watch, so you can see whether a settings change actually helps instead of guessing. Everyday Lite walks you through reversible battery-saving changes with a checklist to undo them. Battery Tile, runtime complication and a 7-day timeline. Runs on the watch, no account. It can't fix a worn-out battery, and sometimes the evidence says that's the real problem.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Will WristRevive change my system settings on its own?

No. Everyday Lite gives you a checklist and you choose what to change; it does not silently modify protected system settings.

> WristRevive does not silently modify protected system settings.

### Can I see battery info without opening the app?

Yes. Battery information is available from a Wear OS Tile or a watch-face complication.

> Check battery information from a Wear OS Tile or watch-face complication.

### Can I export my battery data?

Yes. The phone companion lets you export diagnostics as JSON or CSV when you choose.

> export diagnostics as JSON or CSV when you choose

### How long does a Revive Scan take?

You choose 12 hours or 24 hours. The scan looks at how the battery behaves over that whole period.

> Choose a 12-hour or 24-hour scan to examine battery behavior over time.

### Can it tell me which app is draining my battery?

No. It does not measure exact power use by individual apps. It shows when drain ran higher than your watch's usual baseline, which you can compare with what you were doing.

> identify exact power use by individual apps

### Can WristRevive fix an old battery?

No. It can't repair an ageing battery or guarantee longer battery life. Sometimes what the evidence shows is that the battery itself is the problem.

> It can't fix a worn-out battery, and sometimes the evidence says that's the real problem.

### Why does my result say inconclusive?

Because there wasn't enough evidence for a firm answer. WristRevive labels that result inconclusive instead of presenting a guess as fact.

> When a result lacks sufficient evidence, it is marked inconclusive rather than presented as a definitive answer.

### What if I charge or restart the watch during a scan?

The scan accounts for interruptions like charging, reboots and software changes, so plugging in doesn't silently spoil the result.

> WristRevive accounts for interruptions such as charging, reboots and software changes.

### Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks additional Revive Scans and the Idle Drain Test.

> A one-time Pro purchase unlocks additional Revive Scans and Idle Drain Test.

### What can I use without paying?

Current battery information, recent history, evidence of higher-than-usual drain and the Everyday Lite checklist all work without Pro.

> View current battery information, recent history, supported elevated-drain evidence and Everyday Lite without Pro.

### Do I need an Android phone to use WristRevive?

No. The measurements happen on a supported Wear OS watch. The Android phone app is an optional companion for viewing history and results on a bigger screen.

> The Android phone companion is optional and synchronizes with your paired watch through Wear OS Data Layer.

### What is Everyday Lite?

A checklist of optional display and notification changes that can lower drain. You pick what to change and can switch back with a restoration checklist.

> Follow a reversible checklist of optional display and notification adjustments.

### Does a cancelled scan use up my free scan?

No. A scan that is cancelled, interrupted or ends without enough data does not count.

> A scan that is cancelled, interrupted or ends without enough data does not use it up.

### How long does the Idle Drain Test take?

About two hours in total: a one-hour baseline, then a matched one-hour comparison after you change one thing.

> Measure a one-hour low-interaction baseline, change one setting or behavior, then run a matched one-hour comparison.

### Can it tell how long my battery will last today?

Yes, once it has enough observations. It then shows an estimated remaining runtime.

> See an estimated remaining runtime when enough observations are available.

### Can it give me a battery health score?

No. WristRevive cannot certify battery health or repair an ageing battery.

> WristRevive cannot repair an ageing battery, certify battery health

### Should I buy Pro on the watch or the phone?

Either works. Use the same Google Play account on both, because the watch checks the purchase with Google Play.

> You can buy Pro on your watch or phone; your watch checks the purchase with Google Play, so use the same Google Play account on both.

### Do I need a WristRevive account?

No. No account is required, on the watch or on the phone.

> No WristRevive account is required.

### Can it prove a software update caused my drain?

No. Update comparisons, when available, show an association, not proof of cause.

> Software-update comparisons, when available, show associations rather than proof of cause.

### Which watches does WristRevive work on?

It needs a supported Wear OS watch, because the battery measurements are taken on the watch itself.

> A supported Wear OS watch is required for battery measurements.

### What does the Idle Drain Test show me?

The drain rate in each of the two hours, how big the difference is, and how confident the result is.

> Review the observed drain rates, size of the difference and confidence in the result.

### Can I see when my watch drained faster than usual?

Yes. It marks periods of unusually fast drain compared with your own watch's normal baseline.

> Identify elevated-drain periods against your watch's own baseline.

### Does the phone app measure the battery too?

No. The watch does the measuring; the phone app shows the results on a bigger screen and helps you read them.

> The watch measures battery behavior; the phone displays and helps interpret those measurements.

### How does the phone get the watch data?

The optional companion app syncs with your paired watch through Wear OS Data Layer, and it shows when data was last synced.

> synchronizes with your paired watch through Wear OS Data Layer

### How far back does the battery timeline go?

The launch notes describe a 7-day timeline, alongside a battery Tile and a runtime complication.

> Battery Tile, runtime complication and a 7-day timeline.

### Can I undo the Everyday Lite changes?

Yes. There is a restoration checklist for switching each change back when you want.

> use the restoration checklist when you want to switch back

### Can I see my past charging sessions?

Yes. In the phone companion you can look through charging periods together with scan and test results.

> Explore charging periods, Revive Scan results and Idle Drain comparisons

### What does Pro add over the free version?

More Revive Scans and the Idle Drain Test. It is a one-time purchase.

> A one-time Pro purchase unlocks additional Revive Scans and Idle Drain Test.

### Is there an iPhone companion app?

No. The companion in the listing is for Android phones, and it is optional.

> The Android phone companion is optional and synchronizes with your paired watch through Wear OS Data Layer.

### What does it compare my battery drain against?

Your own watch. High-drain periods are judged against your watch's own baseline, not a general figure.

> Identify elevated-drain periods against your watch's own baseline.

### Why don't I see a runtime estimate yet?

The estimate appears once enough observations are available. Give it some normal wear first.

> See an estimated remaining runtime when enough observations are available.

### Can I test a habit instead of a setting?

Yes. The Idle Drain Test lets you change one setting or behavior between the baseline hour and the matched hour.

> Measure a one-hour low-interaction baseline, change one setting or behavior, then run a matched one-hour comparison.

### Can I tell how fresh the data on my phone is?

Yes. The companion app shows when watch data was last synchronized.

> see when watch data was last synchronized

### Do I have to apply every Everyday Lite change?

No. You choose which changes to make from the checklist.

> Choose what to change and use the restoration checklist when you want to switch back.

### Will my results match someone else's watch?

Not necessarily. Results depend on the watch, how you use it and the quality of the observations.

> Results depend on the watch, your usage and the quality of the available observations.

### How much does WristRevive Pro cost?

$1.99, paid once, on the US Google Play listing according to the developer. It adds the Idle Drain Test and Revive Scans beyond your first free one.

> $1.99 one-time for WristRevive Pro, verified on the live US Google Play listing.

### Does the watch have to be on my wrist during a Revive Scan?

No. The developer says no wear requirement is documented, so a scan can run on a nightstand as long as the watch stays off the charger.

> No worn requirement is documented in the app's docs

### Which test should I use to check drain overnight?

The Idle Drain Test. The developer names it as the dedicated overnight tool, and it needs Pro.

> The dedicated overnight tool is the Idle Drain Test.

### Does the phone keep more battery history than the watch?

Yes. The watch keeps a bounded history, and the phone companion keeps a larger timeline that mirrors the samples the watch has retained.

> the phone keeps a larger timeline as a durable mirror of the watch's retained samples.

### Can I use my watch normally during an Idle Drain Test?

Try not to. The test measures a one-hour low-interaction baseline, then a matched one-hour comparison, so heavy use in either hour makes the two less comparable.

> Measure a one-hour low-interaction baseline, change one setting or behavior, then run a matched one-hour comparison.

### Is WristRevive a one-tap battery saver?

No. It records how your battery behaves and helps you decide what to test next, instead of flipping settings for you.

> Instead of promising a one-tap battery fix, it helps you see what the evidence supports and what to test next.

### What happens if the watch sits on the charger during a whole scan?

It will likely end inconclusive. Charging time does not count as valid observation, so an all-night run on the charger leaves too little evidence.

> an all-night on-charge run would likely end inconclusive (insufficient valid evidence)

### Is there a cap on Revive Scans once I buy Pro?

No. The developer says Pro unlocks unlimited Revive Scans along with the Idle Drain Test.

> It unlocks the Idle Drain Test and unlimited Revive Scans; the first completed Revive Scan is free.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't repair an ageing battery.

  > WristRevive cannot repair an ageing battery

- It doesn't certify battery health, so it is not a battery health report for a repair or resale.

  > certify battery health

- It doesn't measure exact power use by individual apps.

  > identify exact power use by individual apps

- It doesn't measure a phone's battery. Measurements come from a supported Wear OS watch.

  > A supported Wear OS watch is required for battery measurements.

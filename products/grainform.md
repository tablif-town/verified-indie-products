# Grainform

Turn still images into living grain art

- **Site:** [grainform.app](https://grainform.app/)
- **Topic:** [Visual and media](../topics/visual-and-media.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-17

Upload a photo, logo, or type and turn it into responsive grain art in your browser. Shape the material and motion on a canvas, preview it live, and export a still image.

# Fismbot

AI-powered social content and email marketing platform

- **Site:** [fismbot.com](https://fismbot.com/)
- **Topic:** [Marketing and sales](../topics/marketing-and-sales.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-16

Fismbot offers an AI-powered marketing platform that automates social media and email. Post Pilot learns your brand, writes on-brand posts, schedules them across multiple platforms, and keeps feeds active around the clock. Email Pilot creates personalized campaigns using your SMTP, templates, and audience data, with performance analytics. Fismbot also provides done-for-you marketing and custom AI automation services to help you scale faster while cutting costs and manual work.

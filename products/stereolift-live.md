# StereoLift Live

your desktop in real 3D, live on your Quest

- **Site:** [stereolift.live](https://stereolift.live/)
- **Topic:** [Desktop and utilities](../topics/desktop-and-utilities.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-01

Run StereoLift Live on a Windows or Linux PC with an NVIDIA GPU. View your desktop in stereoscopic 3D on Meta Quest over your local network.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Does StereoLift Live require exporting or uploading video files?

No video files need to be exported or uploaded. The app directly captures what is displayed on your PC screen.

> StereoLift Live captures what’s on your PC screen. No files to export or upload.

### Which Linux distribution is supported for installation?

StereoLift Live provides setup and update instructions specifically for Ubuntu systems.

> Install, launch and update on Ubuntu.

### What if my PC has no suitable GPU?

You can convert a video to 3D on stereolift.com instead of running the live app.

> Convert a video at stereolift.com

### What graphics card do I need?

An NVIDIA GPU. The founder recommends at least 8GB of VRAM for 1080p, with an RTX 2080 tested as a low baseline at 30 fps.

> Minimum 8GB VRAM for 1080p (RTX 2080 tested as low baseline at 30 fps)

### Which Quest headsets work with StereoLift Live?

Meta Quest 2, 3, 3S and Pro.

> Meta Quest 2 · 3 · 3S · Pro

### Is StereoLift Live free?

Yes. The site lists free access after you register an account.

> Free access

### Will it work with an AMD or Intel GPU?

Not today. Other GPUs are not ruled out for the future, but there is no date.

> Not excluded but no ETA

### Does my screen go through the cloud?

No. The server runs on your PC and streams over your own network. Cloud services only handle accounts, linked headsets, support and optional diagnostics.

> The server runs on your machine and streams across your own LAN.

### Is there a delay in 3D mode?

Yes. A short processing buffer adds a few seconds, so 2D mode is the one to use when you need a quick response.

> A short processing buffer adds a few seconds of delay, so use 2D when you need a quick response.

### How do I send logs if something breaks?

Open the tray menu of the desktop app, choose Diagnostics and Save Support Bundle, then email the file to support.

> open its tray menu and select Diagnostics → Save Support Bundle, then confirm

### Can I use StereoLift Live with a Mac?

No. It runs on a Windows or Linux PC with an NVIDIA GPU, and macOS is not listed.

> Run StereoLift Live on a Windows or Linux PC with an NVIDIA GPU.

### Can I type and click on my desktop while wearing the headset?

Yes, in 2D mode. You can point, click, scroll and type on your PC from inside the Quest.

> Point, click, scroll, and type with responsive desktop control in your headset.

### Can I move or resize the screen inside the headset?

Yes. You grab the virtual screen with tracked input and change its position, size and angle.

> Move, resize, and angle the virtual screen using tracked input in the headset.

### Do I need an account to use it?

Yes. You register a StereoLift account; cloud services handle sign-in, linked headsets, support and optional diagnostics, while the screen stream itself stays on your network.

> Cloud services manage account access, linked headsets, support, and optional diagnostics.

### Is the stream between my PC and Quest encrypted?

Yes. The picture travels from your PC to your Quest encrypted over your local network.

> Encrypted over your local network

### Can I darken the room around the virtual screen?

Yes. The headset recordings show an option to dim your surroundings, next to the visual settings and effect toggles.

> Dim your surroundings

### What should I include when I report a problem?

Your PC operating system, GPU and Quest model, what you were doing and any error message you saw.

> Mention your PC operating system, GPU and Quest model, what you were doing and any error message.

### Can I convert a video file instead of my live screen?

Not with StereoLift Live, which works on what is on your screen. For video files the site points to the separate StereoLift service.

> Convert a video at stereolift.com

### What frame rate can I expect on an RTX 2080?

About 30 fps at 1080p. The founder names an RTX 2080 with 8GB VRAM as the low baseline they tested, so a weaker card will likely struggle.

> Minimum 8GB VRAM for 1080p (RTX 2080 tested as low baseline at 30 fps)

### Will 3D mode cost money later?

Yes, that is the plan. The founder says 2D will stay free and 3D will move to a subscription in the future, with no price announced yet.

> 2D will stay free, 3D will be subscription based in the future

### Can I play fast games in 3D mode?

Not well. 3D runs a few seconds behind because of a processing buffer, so anything that needs quick reactions belongs in 2D mode.

> A short processing buffer adds a few seconds of delay, so use 2D when you need a quick response.

### Do my PC and Quest need to be on the same network?

Yes. The picture streams from your PC to the headset over your home network, not over the internet.

> Powered by your NVIDIA GPU, streamed over your home network.

### What do the cloud services handle if the stream stays local?

Only the account side. Sign-in, linked headsets, support and optional diagnostics go through the cloud, while the screen video stays on your LAN.

> Cloud services manage account access, linked headsets, support, and optional diagnostics.

### Do I have to install something on my PC?

Yes. A desktop app runs the server on your own machine, and the Quest connects to it.

> The server runs on your machine and streams across your own LAN.

### Is the 3D picture in full colour?

Yes. The depth estimate is turned into two separate full-colour images, one for each eye, so you are not looking at a red and blue anaglyph.

> That depth becomes two distinct, full-colour views. One for each eye, bringing a sense of space to the picture.

### Where can I ask a setup question?

On Discord or by email. The Discord community takes questions and tips, and support&&#35;64;stereolift.live handles private or account matters.

> For private or account matters, or if you prefer email, write to support&&#35;64;stereolift.live.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't provide real-time interaction without delay in 3D viewing mode.

  > A short processing buffer adds a few seconds of delay, so use 2D when you need a quick response.

- It doesn't export or save video files during screen capture.

  > StereoLift Live captures what’s on your PC screen. No files to export or upload.

- It doesn't send desktop screen data over cloud network servers.

  > The server runs on your machine and streams across your own LAN.

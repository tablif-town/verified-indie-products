# FamilyMirror AI -

Discover who your child looks like more — Mom or Dad -

- **Site:** [familymirrorai.devs.surf](https://familymirrorai.devs.surf/)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)

FamilyMirror AI compares visible facial resemblance between family members using private, browser-based AI. Upload photos to see whether a child resembles Mom or Dad more, with feature-by-feature comparisons for eyes, nose, mouth, and face shape. Photos are processed locally on your device, with no account required.

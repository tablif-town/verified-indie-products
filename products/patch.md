# Patch

A complete pro audio suite for Omarchy

- **Site:** [patchaudio.app](https://patchaudio.app/)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-25

Patch takes over your Omarchy audio: volume, outputs, a mixer and headphone EQ on one real-time engine. Free to download; add DJ decks, studio tools and more as modules. Auto Mix is the one paid add-on.

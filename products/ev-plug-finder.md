# EV Plug Finder

Find the right EV charger. Fast.

- **Site:** [evplugfinder.com](https://evplugfinder.com/)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-27

EV Plug Finder is a free tool that helps EV drivers quickly find compatible charging stations nearby. Beyond just locating chargers, drivers can report whether a charger is available and share what they paid to charge, giving the community more useful, real-world information before they arrive. We built EV Plug Finder to make public charging a little less frustrating and a lot more transparent. Try it at EVPlugFinder.com.

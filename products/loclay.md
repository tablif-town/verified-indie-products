# loclay

Turn Google Maps into your lead generation engine

- **Site:** [loclay.cc](https://loclay.cc/)
- **Topic:** [Marketing and sales](../topics/marketing-and-sales.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-22

loclay helps you find local businesses leads on Google Maps and collect their contact information for cold outreach, prospecting and lead generation. Search by location and business category, loclay gets the businesses, visit business websites, and extract owner names, email addresses, phone numbers, social profiles, and other business details. Export the collected leads for your sales and outreach campaigns.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Does it show Google reviews, photos or popular times?

Only the core listing fields. If you need reviews, photos or popular times, a raw data scraper fits that job better.

> Core listing fields only

### Can I search more than one city at a time?

Yes. You pick a business type and add as many cities as you need, in any of 99 countries, and the search runs live on Google Maps.

> Pick a business type and as many cities as you need, in any of 99 countries, and every search runs live on Google Maps

### Does loclay have an API?

No. Results come out as a CSV export; there is no developer API for now.

> No, CSV export

### Can I pull leads through an API?

Not yet. API and MCP access is listed as coming soon on the paid plans, and for now leads come out as a CSV export.

> API & MCP access COMING SOON

### Can a business ask to be corrected or removed?

Yes. Send the business name and city, and loclay corrects or removes the listing within 5 business days.

> We'll correct or remove the listing within 5 business days.

# Canopy Arabian Astrology

Daily Arabic horoscope and astrology app for iOS and Android

- **Site:** [canopyastro.com](https://www.canopyastro.com/)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-25

Canopy brings Ancient Arabian astrology to your phone with precise, source-based readings. It calculates your natal chart using essential dignities, whole-sign houses, Arabic lots, and the Lot of Fortune, explaining each placement in clear language. You'll see planetary hours, the Moon’s lunar mansion, fixed stars, transits, and electional windows to choose the right moment to act. Learn the tradition through built-in guides while following daily counsel drawn from classical texts.

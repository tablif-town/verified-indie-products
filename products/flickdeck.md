# FlickDeck

Trigger webhooks and Home Assistant with a wrist flick

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.flickdeck](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.flickdeck)
- **Topic:** [Work and office](../topics/work-and-office.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-12

FlickDeck maps deliberate Wear OS gestures (double twist, flick up, flick down) to webhooks and Home Assistant actions. Set up a profile on your phone, sync it to the watch, then arm a short session when you want it, so gesture listening isn't on all day. Cooldowns, confirmations and haptic feedback cut accidental triggers, and a test mode shows gesture confidence. No account, ads, analytics or cloud. Free covers one complete setup; Pro is a one-time unlock.

# 3DJ.app

AI-assisted CAD and 3D creation for makers and agents

- **Site:** [3dj.app](https://www.3dj.app/)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-07

3DJ brings parametric CAD and AI 3D generation into your browser. Create editable CAD scripts, export STEP and STL, or explore text-to-3D and image-to-3D concepts. Local CAD runs in the browser; remote MCP lets agents generate, track and download GLB models.

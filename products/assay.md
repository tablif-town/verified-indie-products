# Assay

Every call gets caught, in or out. What matters gets through

- **Site:** [getassay.io](https://getassay.io/)
- **Topic:** [Work and office](../topics/work-and-office.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-27

Most voice AI is infrastructure: metered by the minute, blind to whether a call's inbound or outbound, because it doesn't care about your business, living off usage instead. At Assay, we care, so we built the finished thing instead: it answers everything that rings in, works your whole outbound list, and scores every call 0–100 automatically; the ones worth your time land on your calendar, the rest don't waste it. To top it off, you can use it, without ever having to touch a dashboard or an api.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Does Assay offer a free trial before committing to a paid tier?

Yes, Assay offers a 7-day free trial on both the Starter and Professional tiers.

> 7-day free trial on Starter and Professional.

### Where is customer call data stored and processed?

Data is hosted on US servers using region-locked Hetzner deployment with no European mirroring or cross-border processing.

> Hetzner US deployment. No EU mirroring; no cross-border processing.

### Can Assay connect with standard calendar and scheduling tools?

Yes, Assay includes native integrations for booking directly into Google Calendar and Outlook.

> Native integrations: Google/Outlook, HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Twilio SMS

### Can I hear the agent before signing up?

Yes. You can call the demo agent on +1 (844) 216-8485 or listen to a recorded inbound call.

> or call our demo agent now: +1 (844) 216-8485

### How fast can it be running?

On the Starter plan the dashboard arrives configured and live in 48 hours. Professional and Enterprise take from 48 hours to 15 days, depending on scope.

> Your dashboard arrives configured and live in 48 hours on Starter.

### Does the agent tell callers it is an AI?

Yes. It tells callers up front that it is your assistant, sticks to your script and hands tricky calls to a real person.

> It tells callers up front that it's your assistant, sticks to your script, and hands anything tricky to a real person.

### What does Assay cost each month?

There are three tiers: Starter at $699 a month, Professional at $4,499 and Enterprise at $24,999. Professional and Enterprise also carry a one-time setup fee.

> FEATURE STARTER · $699 PROFESSIONAL · $4,499 ENTERPRISE · $24,999

### What happens if I use more minutes than my plan includes?

You pay overage, billed as one line at the end of the month. The first 500 minutes over the cap cost $0.35 a minute, and the rate gets lower as usage grows.

> Overage is billed in one line at month-end. The rate steps down as volume increases

### Will I get a warning before overage starts?

Yes. Assay alerts you at 80% of your plan cap, in the dashboard and by email, so there is time to upgrade first.

> We alert at 80% of your cap.

### Is there a discount for paying yearly?

Yes. An annual commitment takes 18% off any tier.

> Annual commit discount

### Can I keep my existing business phone number?

Yes. Number porting is included, and each extra dedicated number costs $25 a month.

> US and Canada · CCPA compliant · Number porting included

### How many calls can run at the same time?

Starter runs 3 parallel lines and Professional runs 8. Enterprise has unlimited lines, managed by the Assay team.

> Parallel lines 3 8 Unlimited (managed)

### How long are call recordings kept?

Call audio is deleted after 90 days by default, or after 7 days if you ask. Transcripts and scores stay until you delete them.

> Call audio purged. Transcripts & scores retained until you delete them.

### How big can an outbound list be?

Starter caps a list at 5,000 contacts and Professional at 40,000. Enterprise has no hard cap.

> List size, hard cap 5,000 40,000 None

### Does the Starter plan answer inbound calls?

No. Inbound auto-answer comes with Professional and Enterprise; Starter covers the outbound batch dialer.

> Inbound auto-answer — ✓ ✓

### What is the Assay Score?

A number from 0 to 100 that every call gets, stored with the reasons behind it. It is weighted across fit, intent, urgency, budget and decision authority.

> Every conversation gets a single number, 0–100, with the reasoning behind it.

### How does it decide which leads are hot?

By thresholds you can set per campaign. The example on the site is hot at 80 and above, warm from 50 to 79 and cold below 50.

> Configurable thresholds per campaign — Hot ≥ 80, Warm 50–79, Cold \< 50

### Can a caller be passed to a real person?

Only on Professional and Enterprise, where live human handoff is included. The agent forwards a call when a script escalation rule fires or when the caller asks.

> Live human handoff — ✓ ✓

### Do I need a developer to set it up?

No. Assay sets the agent up for you, and Starter goes live in 48 hours without code.

> Live in 48 hours\*, no code.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't include automated inbound call answering on the Starter tier.

  > Inbound auto-answer — ✓ ✓

- It doesn't provide Business Associate Agreements for HIPAA compliance until its HIPAA Enterprise workflow launches.

  > A Business Associate Agreement is not available until the HIPAA Enterprise workflow ships.

- It doesn't support non-English language calls during live phone conversations.

  > Multilingual support — — English + Spanish (roadmap)

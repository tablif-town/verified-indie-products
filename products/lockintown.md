# LockinTown

Cozy 3D study rooms where the world locks in together

- **Site:** [lockintown.app](https://lockintown.app/)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-03

A free virtual study room. Pick a cozy 3D library or cafe, set a goal, start a focus timer and work beside people from around the world. No camera or mic. Build a streak, style your character and focus with friends

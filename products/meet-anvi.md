# Meet Anvi

Fun offline preschool learning

- **Site:** [meetanvi.com](https://meetanvi.com/)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)

Meet Anvi is an ad-free early learning app for kids 3–8 built for active play, not passive screen time. Key features: • Interactive Learning: A–Z phonics, letter tracing, shape matching, counting, drawing, and read-aloud stories with star rewards. • Parent Dashboard: PIN-protected area with screen-time limits, multiple child profiles, and progress tracking. • Pure Privacy: Zero ads, no third-party tracking SDKs, and offline-first activities—a clean, safe space built around children and parents.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Can I limit how long my child uses the app?

Yes. The parent area, behind a PIN, has a daily time limit, a weekly summary and a skills heat map.

> Behind a PIN: a weekly summary, a skills heat map, and a daily time limit.

### How much does Meet Anvi cost?

There are weekly, monthly and yearly subscriptions: $1.99 a week, $5.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

> $39.99 a year

### Is there an Android version?

Not yet. For now Meet Anvi is on iPhone and iPad, and an Android version is being built.

> Not yet. It's being built. For now Meet Anvi is on iPhone and iPad.

### Does it teach phonics or only letter names?

Phonics. Tapping a letter plays its pure sound, like /b/ as in ball, and children match letters to the sounds that start words.

> Tapping each letter speaks the pure phonemic sound (e.g., /b/ as in ball, /s/ as in sun) without loud musical distortion.

### Can each of my children have their own profile?

Yes. Every child gets a profile with their own avatar, level and progress, and one Anvi Plus plan covers all the children on your account.

> Each child gets their own profile, avatar, level and progress.

### What does the app store about my family?

The parent's email, the account's display name, an optional phone number, and each child's profile and progress so it can sync. There are no ad trackers or third-party analytics in the app.

> The parent's email, the account's display name, an optional phone number, and each child's profile and progress, so it can sync.

### Which ages is Meet Anvi for?

Children aged 3 to 6, from preschool through LKG and UKG.

> LEARNING APP FOR AGES 3–6

### Does my child need to read to use it?

No. Everything is spoken aloud, so children who cannot read yet can still play.

> Everything is spoken aloud, so they don't need to read yet.

### Does it work offline?

Yes. Learning works offline.

> Learning works offline

### Which iPhone and iPad versions does it need?

iOS 17.6 or later. It works with the device held upright or sideways.

> Meet Anvi supports iPhone and iPad running iOS 17.6 or later in both portrait and landscape orientations.

### Does it follow the LKG and UKG curriculum?

Yes. Its three levels match Preschool, LKG and UKG and are aligned with the NCF-FS foundational framework.

> aligned with the NCF-FS foundational framework

### How many stories come with the app?

60 illustrated read-aloud stories in three reading levels. Children can tap any word to hear it.

> Meet Anvi includes 60 illustrated read-aloud stories in three progressive reading tiers.

### Can my child trace with an Apple Pencil?

Yes. On supported iPads, letters and numbers can be traced with a finger or an Apple Pencil.

> Children can trace letters and numbers using their finger or an Apple Pencil on supported iPad devices.

### Does the Thinking feature record my child's voice?

No. Speech turns into text on the device, no audio is kept, and the words never leave the phone or tablet. You can read or delete every entry in the parent area.

> no audio is recorded or kept, and your child's words never leave it

### Which languages does the app come in?

Menus and instructions come in 11 languages, including English, Dutch, French, German, Japanese and Spanish. The 60 stories are in English.

> 60 illustrated read-aloud stories in English with tap-to-hear words. The app's menus and instructions are available in 11 languages.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't have an Android version yet; it runs on iPhone and iPad only.

  > Not yet. It's being built. For now Meet Anvi is on iPhone and iPad.

- It doesn't run on iPhones or iPads older than iOS 17.6.

  > iPhone and iPad running iOS 17.6 or later, in portrait and landscape.

- It doesn't offer the read-aloud stories in languages other than English.

  > 60 illustrated read-aloud stories in English with tap-to-hear words.

- It doesn't include Math Wizard or Thinking in the free version; they need Anvi Plus.

  > Anvi Plus is an optional subscription that adds exclusive features, such as Thinking and Math Wizard.

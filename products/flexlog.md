# FlexLog

Log sets, reps, weights and rest timers from your wrist

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flexlog](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flexlog)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)

FlexLog keeps strength-training logging on your wrist. Track exercises, sets, reps and weight, run rest timers, reuse workout templates and review history. It works standalone on Wear OS, with optional phone sync and a one-time Pro upgrade.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Do I have to create a user account?

No, you can log your workouts without an account, as the app works directly on the watch.

> Core workout logging does not require a phone or an account.

### Does the app include a timer for breaks between sets?

Yes, you can enable an automatic rest timer that starts counting down immediately after you log a set.

> Enable the automatic rest timer to begin a countdown after each logged set.

### Can I access the app from my watch face?

Yes, you can use Tiles or complications to start sessions or check your current exercise progress.

> Add the FlexLog Tile to start a workout, resume an active session, check the current exercise and set

### What history features are unlocked with the Pro version?

The Pro upgrade provides access to your complete workout and exercise history instead of just the last seven days.

> A one-time FlexLog Pro purchase unlocks: • Unlimited workout templates • Complete workout and exercise history • Custom exercises

### How many workout templates can I save for free?

The free version allows you to create and reuse one custom workout template.

> The free version includes active workout logging, one reusable custom workout template, and the last seven days of workout history.

### Can I change exercises while I am in the middle of a workout?

Yes, the app allows you to switch to a different exercise during an active session.

> Exercise switching during an active workout

### How does the app use watch notifications?

The app uses notification permissions to show your active workout status and rest-timer progress.

> Notification access is used for active workout and rest-timer status when enabled.

### Can I skip a rest timer before the countdown finishes?

Yes, you can check the remaining time on your watch and skip the rest period whenever you are ready to proceed.

> Check the remaining time on your watch, skip the rest period when you are ready, or return to the active exercise for the next set.

### Where is my workout data stored?

Your core workout data is saved directly on your watch.

> Core workout data is stored locally on the watch.

### What type of watch is required?

The app is built specifically for compatible watches running the Wear OS platform.

> FlexLog runs directly on a compatible Wear OS watch.

### Is there a recurring subscription fee?

No, the app does not charge a subscription fee; it offers a one-time purchase for Pro features.

> There is no subscription.

### Can FlexLog be used to monitor my health?

No. It is made for logging strength training: exercises, sets, reps, weight and rest. It is not a medical or health monitoring app.

> FlexLog is built for strength-training workout logging, not medical monitoring.

### Can I fix a set I logged by mistake?

On the phone you can repeat or correct entries during a session, then look back at the finished workout afterwards.

> Build routines, track your planned sets, repeat or correct entries and review past sessions.

### Is there a cloud account that keeps my data in sync across devices?

No. There is no FlexLog cloud and no automatic backup between devices. Each log lives on the device where you created it.

> FlexLog does not provide a developer-hosted workout cloud or automatic cross-device history backup.

### Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Logging a session does not need a connection, which matters in gyms where the signal drops as soon as you walk downstairs.

> core logging does not require an internet connection

### Can I export my workout log?

From the phone app, yes. You can export the workouts you logged on the phone as a JSON file and import that file again later.

> Manually export or import your phone-created workout journal using a JSON file

### Is the watch to phone sync a backup of my history?

No. The phone shows recent watch summaries and template names in read-only form, which is handy for a quick look but does not replace the history stored on the watch.

> The synced watch summaries are not a full backup of watch history.

### Will workouts I log on my phone show up on my watch?

No. Phone workouts stay on the phone. The connection only goes one way, so the phone can show recent watch summaries but the watch never receives phone sessions.

> Phone-created workouts remain separate and are not sent to the watch.

### Can I use FlexLog on an Android phone without a watch?

Yes. The phone app is a full workout log of its own, so you can build routines and record sets even if you never pair a watch.

> Log workouts directly on your Android phone, whether or not you own a watch.

### Is FlexLog free to download?

Yes. It is free on Google Play. An optional one-time Pro purchase adds more templates, custom exercises and older history on the watch.

> Price: Free

### How much watch history can I see without paying?

The last seven days. Older workout and exercise history on the watch comes with FlexLog Pro.

> access to the last seven days of watch workout history

### Can I add my own exercises on the watch?

Only with Pro. The one-time Pro purchase unlocks custom exercises on the watch, along with more custom templates.

> unlocks additional custom templates, custom exercises and older workout and exercise history on the watch

### Can I pause a workout and pick it up again?

Yes. During an active session on the watch you can pause, resume and switch exercises, then review what you finished.

> switch exercises, pause or resume, and review what you completed

### Does the phone app come with ready-made routines?

Yes. On the Android phone you can start from a built-in routine or build your own and track the planned sets.

> Start from a built-in routine or create your own

### Can I see my watch templates on my phone?

Yes, if FlexLog is installed on both. The phone shows recent watch workout summaries and the names of your watch templates.

> view recent watch workout summaries and watch template names in the phone app

### Can I edit a watch workout from the phone?

No. Watch information is read-only on the phone, so corrections to watch sessions happen on the watch.

> Watch information is read-only on the phone.

### Does it keep track of my heaviest lifts?

Yes. Next to workout and exercise history, the watch keeps heaviest-weight records for your exercises.

> Review workout history, exercise history and heaviest-weight records

## What it does not do

- It doesn't store more than seven days of history in the free version.

  > The free version includes active workout logging, one reusable custom workout template, and the last seven days of workout history.

- It doesn't allow more than one reusable custom template in the free version.

  > The free version includes active workout logging, one reusable custom workout template

- It doesn't support smartwatches that do not use the Wear OS platform.

  > FlexLog runs directly on a compatible Wear OS watch.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Do users need to create an account to use the calculators?

No account or registration is required. You can access all material calculators immediately without providing an email address or signing up.

> No sign-up, no email.

### How does BuildRuler help verify input measurements?

Each calculator displays its formula and generates a scale drawing of your project. This allows you to visually sanity-check your inputs before ordering materials.

> Each calculator shows its formula, draws your project to scale so you can sanity-check the input

### How many calculators are available on the platform?

BuildRuler offers 80 calculators designed for landscaping, concrete, carpentry, electrical, and cost estimation projects.

> 80 free calculators for mulch, gravel, concrete, lumber, etc

### Can I use my own supplier's prices?

Yes. You can swap the typical material weights and prices for the figures your supplier gives you.

> lets you replace our typical figures (material weights, prices) with your supplier's.

### Are the results exact enough to order from?

No. They are planning estimates, so check your measurements, local codes and supplier figures before you buy or build.

> Results are estimates for planning. Check measurements, local codes and supplier figures before you buy or build.

### Can I see how a number was worked out?

Yes. Each calculator shows the formula it uses, so you can follow the math behind the result.

> Each calculator shows its formula

### Is BuildRuler free?

Yes. The calculators are free and the site is paid for by ads.

> The site is supported by ads.

### Can I use it on my phone at the store or on site?

Yes. The founder confirms every calculator works in a mobile browser with nothing to install.

> Are all calculators usable on mobile web browsers without installing software?

### Will it tell me how many bags of concrete to buy?

Yes. The concrete calculator gives cubic yards and bag counts for 40 to 80 lb bags, for slabs, footings, columns and post holes.

> Yards and 40–80 lb bags for slabs, footings, columns and post holes.

### Does the gravel calculator give tons or cubic yards?

Both. It works out tons and yards of gravel for driveways, paths and beds.

> Tons and yards of gravel for driveways, paths and beds.

### Can it estimate what a whole job will cost?

Yes, for some jobs. There are cost estimators for things like a concrete slab, a roof replacement, gutters and driveways.

> Installed slab cost by size, thickness, finish and rebar.

### Can I work in metric?

Yes, in several calculators. There are converters between inches and millimeters and a square meters calculator with sq ft to m² conversion.

> Square meters from length × width, and sq ft ↔ m².

### Does it cover electrical work too?

Yes. There are electrical calculators, including one for voltage drop and wire size on a run.

> Voltage drop and the right wire size for a run.

# Fashion Try-On AI

Turn fabric into outfits and virtually try them on with AI

- **Site:** [fashiontryon.vishnexa.com](https://fashiontryon.vishnexa.com/)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-31

Fashion Try-On AI by VISHNEXA turns fabric or clothing images into AI-generated outfit concepts and lets you virtually try them on using a person photo. Explore different fashion styles, visualize garments before making or buying them, save your creations, and prepare outfit details for tailoring. Available on the web and Android.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### What do I need to upload for a try-on?

Two images: a photo of the person and a garment or material reference. You pick a style such as Maxi Dress or Co-ord Set and get both a standalone outfit and the outfit shown on the person.

> Upload a person photo and a garment or material reference, select the fashion style you want, and generate two polished results: the finished outfit and the outfit applied to the person.

### Can I start without a fabric or garment photo?

Yes. You can begin from a written description of the garment, silhouette, color and occasion, then try the generated outfit on a person photo.

> Begin with a written description, a material image, a garment reference or an outfit you have already created.

### Can I hand a design to my tailor?

Yes, as a starting point. Fashion PDF Export puts the concept, try-on visuals and tailoring notes into a PDF you can share with a designer or tailor.

> Export selected fashion information into structured PDF documents for personal reference, presentation and discussion with designers or tailors.

### Do I need a fabric photo, or can I start from a written idea?

Either works. You can start from a written description, a material image, a garment reference or an outfit you already made in the app.

> Begin with a written description, a material image, a garment reference or an outfit you have already created.

### What happens to the person photos I upload?

They stay tied to your account. The site says your account, uploaded references and generated outfits are kept protected.

> Your accounts, uploaded references and generated fashion assets stay protected.

### Is there an iPhone app?

No, not yet. It runs on the web and on Android.

> The app runs on the web and Android, with no iPhone app yet.

### Will the real garment look exactly like the preview?

No. A finished garment can differ in color, fit, drape and construction, so use the preview as a guide.

> Final physical garments can differ in color, fit, drape and construction.

### Can I ask questions about one specific outfit?

Yes. You can ask about the outfit you are looking at and get practical guidance on it.

> Ask context-aware questions about the specific outfit and receive practical guidance.

### Will the try-on keep my face and pose?

It tries to. The system aims to preserve the person's identity, pose and general appearance, though results vary with the photo.

> The system aims to preserve the person’s identity, pose and general appearance.

### Which outfit styles can I pick?

Several. The style list includes jumpsuit, bodycon dress, co-ord set and maxi dress, and the home page also shows midi, mini and kurti styles.

> Jumpsuit Bodycon Dress Co-ord Set Maxi Dress

### How do I get a better try-on result?

Use a well-lit photo where your face and body are visible, and keep hands, bags and hair away from the clothing area.

> Keep hands, bags, hair and other objects from covering important body or garment areas.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't guarantee the real garment will match the preview. Color, fit, drape and construction can all come out differently.

  > Final physical garments can differ in color, fit, drape and construction.

- It doesn't replace a tailor or pattern maker. Its measurements, patterns and production notes are meant to be checked by one.

  > Measurements, patterns and production outputs should be reviewed by a qualified tailor or pattern maker.

- It doesn't have an iPhone app listed. It runs on the web and Android.

  > Available on the web and Android.

- It doesn't give the same result every time. Output shifts with image quality, pose, garment complexity and lighting.

  > Results can vary depending on image quality, instructions, pose, garment complexity and lighting.

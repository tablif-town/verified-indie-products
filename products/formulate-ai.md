# Formulate AI

An AI workspace where people and agents run work together

- **Site:** [formulateai.in](https://www.formulateai.in/)
- **Topic:** [AI and agents](../topics/ai-and-agents.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-20

Formulate AI helps teams run work through a conversational AI assistant, specialised agents and shared company memory. Delegate tasks, coordinate people and agents, and keep humans in control with approvals. Built for founders and lean teams that want to scale execution without adding more operational overhead.

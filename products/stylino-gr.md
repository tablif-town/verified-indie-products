# Stylino GR

One search, every fashion store shipping to Greece

- **Site:** [stylino.gr](https://stylino.gr/)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)

Fashion-only price comparison for Greece. Stylino indexes \~686k products from 4,400+ brands across 79 stores that ship to Greece, matching the same item across retailers so every offer sits on one page, lowest price first. Set a price alert on any product or follow a brand, category or store for new arrivals, with no account needed. Fully bilingual (EL/EN) with buying guides tied to live prices. Free for shoppers; ranking is never influenced by commissions.

# MotionGuard

A motion alarm for your phone when you step away

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.motionguard](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.motionguard)
- **Topic:** [Desktop and utilities](../topics/desktop-and-utilities.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-23

Arm MotionGuard before leaving your phone on a desk, charger or hotel nightstand, and it sounds a local alarm if the phone is moved. Free includes Normal sensitivity, a settle delay, a sensitivity test and local trigger history. Pro adds charger-removal alerts, Low/High sensitivity and best-effort Wear OS wrist alerts, as a one-time purchase or an optional 8-hour pass from a rewarded ad. No account, and no location, camera or mic permission. It alerts; it doesn't prevent theft.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Will an ad ever interrupt an armed alarm?

No. Rewarded ads only appear when you ask for them, and never while MotionGuard is arming, armed, alarming or disarming.

> Rewarded ads are not shown automatically and are not shown while MotionGuard is arming, armed, alarming, or disarming.

### How do I get Pro without paying?

You can watch an optional rewarded ad for an 8-hour Advanced Guard Pass, or make a one-time lifetime purchase. There is no subscription.

> Unlock Pro with an optional rewarded ad for an 8-hour Advanced Guard Pass after server verification, or make a one-time lifetime purchase.

### Does MotionGuard need location, camera or microphone access?

No. Core motion monitoring runs on the phone and does not ask for location, camera or microphone permission.

> does not request Android location, camera, or microphone permission

### Will it behave the same on every Android phone?

Not always. Android power management, the phone's hardware, audio policy and connectivity can all affect how it behaves, so run a test on your own phone first.

> Android power management, device hardware, audio policy, and connectivity can affect behavior.

### Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no MotionGuard account and no MotionGuard cloud service.

> MotionGuard does not require an account or a MotionGuard cloud service.

### Does it keep a recording of my phone's movement?

No. The trigger history keeps coarse movement evidence for each alarm, and continuous raw accelerometer traces are not retained.

> Continuous raw accelerometer traces are not retained.

### Can it track my phone if someone takes it?

No. MotionGuard sounds an alarm on the phone. It does not track a device or prevent theft.

> It does not prevent theft, track a device, provide emergency monitoring, or guarantee remote alerts.

### How do I turn the alarm off when I come back?

Disarm it from the app or from the ongoing notification. The app keeps the armed state visible while it is monitoring.

> MotionGuard keeps the active monitoring state visible and can be disarmed from the app or ongoing notification.

### What does Pro add?

An alarm when the charger is pulled out, Low and High sensitivity presets, and trigger alerts on a paired Wear OS watch that are sent when the connection allows.

> MotionGuard Pro adds charger-removal monitoring, Low and High sensitivity, and best-effort Wear OS trigger alerts.

### What does the free version include?

The motion alarm with Normal sensitivity, settle-delay controls, a sensitivity test and local trigger history.

> The free core includes Normal sensitivity, settle-delay controls, a sensitivity test, and local trigger history.

### Is MotionGuard a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play, and there is no subscription.

> There is no subscription.

### How much does MotionGuard Pro cost?

US $2.99 as a one-time purchase, according to the founder. Google Play sets regional prices in other countries.

> US $2.99 one-time (Google Play sets regional prices in other countries).

### What is the settle delay for?

It gives you time to put the phone down after you tap Arm. The phone settles first, then monitoring starts, so your own hand does not set it off.

> Choose a settle delay, tap Arm, and let the phone settle.

### Can I check the sensitivity before I rely on it?

Yes. A visible sensitivity test shows how the phone reacts to movement before you arm a full alarm.

> A visible sensitivity test lets you check how the phone reacts to movement before arming a full alarm.

### Will it go off if someone unplugs my charger?

Only with Pro, and only if the phone was connected to power when you armed it. If charging is unavailable, the app shows that state.

> When the phone is connected to power at arm time, MotionGuard Pro can also watch for charger removal.

### Does it work with a Wear OS watch?

Yes. A compatible Wear OS watch shows the phone's latest MotionGuard status and a status Tile. Wrist alerts after a trigger need Pro.

> Pair a compatible Wear OS watch to view the phone's latest known MotionGuard status and access the status Tile.

### Will my watch always get the alert?

No. Watch alerts are not guaranteed and depend on connectivity. The phone alarm is the one to rely on.

> The phone remains authoritative, and watch delivery depends on connectivity.

### Can I delete the trigger history?

Yes. Detailed history stays on the device and you can clear it in the app.

> Detailed history stays on the device and can be cleared in the app.

### Is there an iPhone version?

Not in the sources. MotionGuard is listed on Google Play for Android phones.

> MotionGuard: Motion Alarm - Google Play

## What it does not do

- It doesn't prevent theft. It sounds an alarm when the phone is moved.

  > It does not prevent theft

- It doesn't track or locate a phone.

  > track a device

- It isn't an emergency monitoring service.

  > provide emergency monitoring

- It doesn't guarantee that an alert reaches your watch, because delivery depends on the connection.

  > The phone remains authoritative, and watch delivery depends on connectivity.

# Would You Rather: a form that listens

Build forms with voice. No typing required.

- **Site:** [wyr.humanbit.in](https://wyr.humanbit.in/)
- **Topic:** [Work and office](../topics/work-and-office.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-16

Would You Rather is a form builder with voice on both ends. Describe the form you need and AI drafts it with the right field types. Record each question in your own voice. Respondents answer by speaking from any phone browser, in any language, no app or login. Branching logic, your brand, collaborators, walk-up QR forms and a public API are built in. Every answer lands live in a Google Sheet you own, audio attached. Free to use today.

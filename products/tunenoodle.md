# Tunenoodle

Create AI music from text, lyrics, images or humming. Preview styles, extend or remix songs, and use free BPM, chord, speed, pitch and reverb tools.

- **Site:** [tunenoodle.com](https://tunenoodle.com/)
- **Topic:** [Visual and media](../topics/visual-and-media.md)

Create AI music from text, lyrics, images or humming, then keep working in the same studio: extend it from any point, replace a section, mash two tracks, split it into 2-12 stems, or turn a script into speech. What sets it apart: 13 audio tools run free in the browser with no account, no credits, processed locally. In ten languages.

# Paperwing

The free, open-source alternative to Adobe Acrobat

- **Site:** [github.com/rohanvijaykumar/paperwing](https://github.com/rohanvijaykumar/paperwing)
- **Topic:** [Work and office](../topics/work-and-office.md)

Paperwing is a free, open-source PDF app for Windows and macOS with Acrobat's layout and shortcuts. Edit, sign, convert, organize, compress and protect PDFs with 100+ tools. No account, no subscription, no upsells, and your files never leave your computer.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Can I sign PDFs with a smart card or USB token?

Yes. It signs with Digital IDs from the Windows certificate store, smart cards, USB tokens or the Mac Keychain, and the signatures are valid in Acrobat.

> Digital IDs from Windows' certificate store, smart cards, USB tokens or the Mac Keychain. Valid in Acrobat.

### Does redaction actually remove the hidden text?

Yes. Redaction deletes the content under the box instead of only covering it.

> Redaction really deletes what's under it.

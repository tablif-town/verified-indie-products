# Dispoly

Dispoly: Book vehicles, rooms and equipment in one place

- **Site:** [dispoly.net](https://dispoly.net/)
- **Topic:** [Work and office](../topics/work-and-office.md)

Booking software for shared resources. Vehicles, rooms and equipment in one live plan, with public booking links, approvals and handovers. No more double bookings.

### Do I need a credit card to try it?

No. Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial without a card, and you can cancel anytime.

In the EU. The pricing page lists EU data hosting with GDPR compliance on every plan.

> EU data hosting, GDPR-compliant

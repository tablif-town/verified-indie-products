## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### What kind of Islamic questions can I ask the DeenGuide?

The DeenGuide provides sourced answers for situations where you are unsure what to do or have an Islamic question. It is designed to help you find guidance for your specific situation.

> I have an Islamic question DeenGuide, sourced answers

### How can I support the community using the Dua Circle?

You can see when someone in your vicinity is experiencing a hardship and send them a silent prayer. The app tracks how many silent duas have been sent that day.

> Someone nearby is going through a prolonged hardship today 0 silent duas sent today

### What does logging in with the Pi Network provide?

Logging in with the Pi Network allows you to verify your identity and unlock Pi features. It is not required for tools like prayer times or the dua library.

> Login with Pi Network Verify your identity & unlock Pi features

### What happens when I pick how I'm feeling on the first screen?

The app asks what you are going through, from stressed or anxious to grateful, and then points you to something that fits. If you choose that you need peace, it takes you to reflection, duas and the Sabr timer.

> Reflection, duas, Sabr timer

### Is there anything for someone new to Islam?

Yes. One of the starting options is for people who want to learn, and it leads to beginner guides, the Quran and duas.

> Beginner guides, Quran, duas

### How does the tasbeeh counter work?

You tap to count, so you can keep your dhikr without beads. The morning dhikr item in the tracker follows the familiar count of SubhanAllah 33, Alhamdulillah 33 and AllahuAkbar 34.

> SubhanAllah 33 × Alhamdulillah 33 × AllahuAkbar 34

### How do Sabr Points work?

You earn points for small acts in the app, such as reading a dua, completing an ibadah item or checking in each day. Streaks add a bonus at 7 days and again at 30 days, and points move you from Beginner toward Believer.

> 30-day streak bonus

### What is in the daily ibadah tracker?

Six things to tick off each day: Fajr, Quran recitation of at least one page, Dhuhr and Asr, morning dhikr, Maghrib and Isha, and a personal dua. The days of the current week run along the top.

> 0 of 6 completed

### Do the duas and hadith show where they come from?

Yes, the ones on the home screen do. The sunnah of the day is cited to Sunan Abi Dawud 1522, and the morning dua is marked as from Muslim.

> Sunan Abi Dawud 1522

### Do I have to sign up, and will I see ads?

No to both. Prayer times, the Qibla compass, the dua library and the tasbeeh counter open without a login, and there are no ads.

> no ads, no login.

### Does SabrTime cost anything?

No. The app is free and runs on donations. If you want to support it, you can give any amount by UPI, card or netbanking, but nothing is locked behind it.

> Help keep this app free for the Ummah — any amount, via UPI, card or netbanking

### How can I donate to keep SabrTime free?

Any amount can be given through UPI, card or netbanking from the Support SabrTime button.

> Help keep this app free for the Ummah — any amount, via UPI, card or netbanking

### Is there a verse of the day?

Yes. The home screen shows an Ayah of the Day with Arabic, transliteration and an English meaning, and you can share it.

> AYAH OF THE DAY

### Does it keep a streak?

Yes. A streak counter shows how many days in a row you have checked in.

> days in a row

### Can I track how many pages of Quran I read?

Yes. The daily tracker has a Quran recitation item with a page counter, starting from a goal of one page.

> Read at least 1 page

### What does the morning dhikr item count?

SubhanAllah 33 times, Alhamdulillah 33 times and Allahu Akbar 34 times.

> SubhanAllah 33 × Alhamdulillah 33 × AllahuAkbar 34

### Can I share a dua with someone?

Yes. The daily dua has a share button, and you can move on to the next dua.

> Share This Dua

### Does SabrTime include the 99 Names of Allah?

Yes. The 99 Names are listed among the extra features, next to points and the spiritual score.

> Points, Tap-When-Stressed, 99 Names, Spiritual Score

### Are there ebooks to read?

Yes. There is an ebooks section, and a recent announcement added an ebook called Guidance to Jannah.

> New Ebook Available — Guidance to Jannah!

### Can I see all of today's prayer times, not just the next one?

Yes. The home screen shows the next prayer, and a tap opens all of the day's times.

> Tap to see all times

### Does SabrTime have a Qibla compass?

Yes. A Qibla compass sits next to the prayer times, dua library and tasbeeh counter.

> Free prayer times, Qibla compass, dua library & tasbeeh counter

### Is there something to use in a stressful moment?

Yes. Under the extra features there is a Tap When Stressed option, next to the 99 Names and a spiritual score.

> Points, Tap-When-Stressed, 99 Names, Spiritual Score

### Are DeenGuide's answers sourced?

Yes. DeenGuide is listed on the home screen as the place for Islamic questions, with sourced answers.

> DeenGuide, sourced answers

### Can I share a dua or reminder with family?

Yes. The sunnah of the day and the daily dua each have a share button, so you can send them on to others.

> Share this reminder

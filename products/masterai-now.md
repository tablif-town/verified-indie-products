# MasterAI Now

Learn ML and LLMs with bite-size lessons and interactive simulations

- **Site:** [masterainow.com](https://masterainow.com/)
- **Topic:** [Education](../topics/education.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-24

MasterAI Now teaches the full machine learning and LLM lifecycle through short, jargon-free cards and browser-based playgrounds. You read concise explanations, check your understanding with quick quizzes, and manipulate live simulations to see concepts in action. The 11-stage track spans from simple linear models to KV-cache behavior in running LLMs. It’s free to learn, and a verifiable completion certificate is available for purchase after finishing every stage.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Do learners need to create an account or register to start?

Learners can access all lessons and simulations without creating an account. Progress is stored locally in the web browser.

> No account, email or personal data is required to learn.

### How can learners prove they completed the full curriculum?

Upon finishing all 11 stages, learners can purchase a cryptographically signed verifiable certificate for $24. This includes a shareable card designed for LinkedIn.

> When every stage is complete you can generate a verifiable certificate with your name and a shareable card for LinkedIn.

### What types of visual simulations are featured in the stages?

The interactive playgrounds include live simulation canvases, 3D vector spaces, and matrices. Parameters can be modified directly within the browser.

> Unlock a live simulation — a canvas, a 3D space or a matrix — and change the parameters yourself.

### Is MasterAI Now free?

Yes. The 11-stage track is free.

> 11 stages · readers + live simulations · free

### How long does the whole course take?

About three hours across 11 stages, with each stage taking 11 to 15 minutes.

> FREE FOREVER 3h

### What does one stage include?

Five short reader cards, a one-click check after each card and a live simulation you can play with.

> Five cards written for humans: plain language, bold key terms, one honest analogy each.

### Is the course available in languages other than English?

Partly. The site offers several interface languages, but translations are machine-assisted and still open to human review, so some content may stay in English.

> Machine-assisted translation, open to human review.

### Does the site show ads?

Yes, it can. It uses Google AdSense to keep the lessons free, and analytics and ads stay off until you accept them in the cookie notice.

> We use Google Analytics to see which lessons actually help people learn, and Google AdSense to keep the lessons free.

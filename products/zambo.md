# Zambo

The verifiable work layer for AI

- **Site:** [zambo.dev](https://zambo.dev/)
- **Topic:** [AI and agents](../topics/ai-and-agents.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-11

Your AI said it did the work. Here's the receipt. Zambo is the verifiable work layer for AI: 153 tools behind one MCP endpoint, zambo.dev/mcp (36 on the default surface), continuity across AIs, and a verifiable AER-1 receipt for every job proving the saved result wasn't changed. Flagship HANDOVER. 123,856 receipts issued; 216,307 downloads. Solo-built by Brennan Zambo. Free 20 calls + 1 wrap-up, pickup free. Day Pass $0.99/24h; Zambo Pass $29/30 days ($19 for $ZAMBO holders); Lifetime $250.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### How long does Zambo keep my task notes and memory?

Your notes follow you across different AI applications for the duration of your pass plus an additional 30 days.

> Notes follow across AI apps through the pass plus 30 days

### Do I need to create an account to use a Day Pass?

No account is required to use Zambo. You can purchase access via card or PayPal, and individual tool or provider limits may still apply.

> No account needed; individual tool and provider limits may apply

### How do I connect Zambo to my AI assistant?

You copy the provided MCP URL and paste it into your AI's connector settings. The process is free, requires no API key, and takes about 10 seconds.

> Copy → paste into your AI's connectors → done. Free, no key, \~10 seconds.

### What can I do for free each day?

Every tool once a day, plus 3 continue calls and one wrap-up handover. Picking a handover up in another AI stays free, and free access resets at 00:00 UTC.

> Every tool free once a day, plus 3 continue calls. One free wrap-up handover per UTC day; pickup stays free.

### Which AI apps does it work with?

Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Grok are named on the site, and VS Code is listed as a connection option. You add one MCP link to the app's connectors.

> add one link to claude, chatgpt, cursor or grok.

### Will a pass renew and charge me again?

No. Day Pass ($0.99 for 24 hours) and Zambo Pass ($29 for 30 days) are single payments with no automatic renewal.

> One payment. No automatic renewal. Same access on both passes.

### What is the difference between Day Pass and Zambo Pass?

Only the length. Both unlock the same access, one for 24 hours and one for 30 days.

> Day Pass and Zambo Pass unlock identical access. Choose 24 hours or 30 days, with the same feature set.

### Does a receipt prove the AI's answer is right?

No. A receipt shows the saved result has not changed since the step ran, and what ran and what came back. Judging whether the answer is correct is still up to you.

> A receipt does not prove the answer was correct; it shows what ran and what came back.

### Does my client need an account to check a Handover?

The client gets one page and checks every step in the browser, then accepts or flags the work. Receipt verification and client decisions stay free.

> Client decisions and receipt verification stay free.

### How do I pay?

By card or PayPal at checkout, or with USDC on Base. Funded AI agents can also buy the Day Pass themselves.

> Pay by card or PayPal. Checkout opens here; available methods appear when supported by the payment processor.

### Is there a lifetime option?

Yes. A separate $250 one-time Lifetime Pass covers current Zambo Pass features while Zambo operates. It does not promise future features.

> $250 once for current Zambo Pass features while Zambo operates. Future features and third-party AI/provider costs are excluded.

### How many price alerts or watchers can I keep running for free?

One at a time. Free access allows a single active watcher, and any active pass makes watchers unlimited.

> 1 active watcher at a time; any active pass unlocks unlimited watchers

### How many tools will my AI actually see?

Your AI sees 37 core tools by default. The full catalog has 153, and your AI can still reach the rest.

> 153 tools. Your AI sees the 37 most useful by default and can reach all of them.

### Can I analyse a long document on the free plan?

Only a first look. Free access includes one completed first-range document preview per day, and continuing the analysis or running private file jobs needs a pass.

> Try one completed first-range document preview per day; continued analysis and private file jobs require a pass.

### What happens if I run out of free calls in the middle of a job?

The request pauses. After you buy a pass, go back to the same AI and retry the paused request yourself, because it does not restart on its own.

> After checkout, return to the same AI and retry the paused request; it does not run automatically.

### Can my AI agent buy a pass without me?

Yes, if the agent is funded. It can buy the Day Pass itself with USDC on Base through agent payment. The Lifetime Pass is human checkout only.

> Funded AI agents can buy the Day Pass themselves with USDC on Base through agent payment.

### Is there a cheaper pass for $ZAMBO holders?

Yes. Holders can get the Zambo Pass for $19 for 30 days, paid once, after verifying their holdings.

> Hold $ZAMBO? Get Zambo Pass for $19 for 30 days, one payment.

### Can I invite other people into a job on my pass?

Yes, through private shared Jobs. The owner's pass covers invited participants, and they get scoped, revocable access instead of access to the owner's account.

> Private shared Jobs; one owner’s pass covers invited participants

### Does picking up work in ChatGPT work on a free ChatGPT plan?

No. Finishing a job in ChatGPT needs a paid ChatGPT plan with developer mode turned on.

> Start in Claude, finish in ChatGPT (paid plans with developer mode).

## What it does not do

- It doesn't cover the costs of third-party AI subscriptions or compute.

  > A Zambo pass does not cover third-party AI subscriptions or provider compute.

- It doesn't guarantee that the AI's output is correct.

  > The saved result is unchanged. That does not prove the answer is right.

- It doesn't promise future features under the Lifetime Pass.

  > This option covers today’s Level 2 benefits only; it does not promise future features

- It doesn't provide a safety guarantee for scam checks.

  > You get: A risk assessment and a receipt, not a safety guarantee.

# Resona

Clone any voice in minutes — pay only for what you use

- **Site:** [resonaio.com](https://resonaio.com/)
- **Topic:** [Visual and media](../topics/visual-and-media.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-06

Resona is an AI voice cloning and text-to-speech platform with no monthly fee — you buy credits and pay only for what you use. Clone a voice from a short sample (250 credits per clone), then generate natural speech in 80+ languages. Also includes voice design and speech-to-text.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Can I use a cloned voice in commercial projects?

Yes, for voices you own or have written consent to use. Commercial use costs the same credits.

> Yes, for voices you own or have written consent to use. Commercial use does not change the credit price.

### Is the text I paste used to train AI models?

No. Input text goes to a technology partner for processing only, and it is not sold or used for training.

> Input text is sent to our technology partner for processing only. It is not sold and not used to train models.

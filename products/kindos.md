# Kindos

Family life, a little easier.

- **Site:** [getkindos.com](https://getkindos.com/)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-20

Kindos is a family organizer app for chores, homework, rewards, shared calendars, family messaging, shopping lists, life skills and age-appropriate AI Tutor help. Launching soon.

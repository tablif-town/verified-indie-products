# Night Crew

Keep your Mac awake only while coding agents work lid closed

- **Site:** [nightcrewapp.com](https://nightcrewapp.com/)
- **Topic:** [AI and agents](../topics/ai-and-agents.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-10

Night Crew is a Mac menu bar app that keeps your Mac awake only while Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Gemini CLI is working, then lets it sleep about a minute after the last agent stops. Lid mode keeps agents running with the lid closed, on the charger by default. Battery, heat and 24-hour guards are on by default, and "Why is my Mac awake?" shows what's holding it up. No account, no analytics. One-time $12 for two Macs, 14-day free trial with no card.

# WalkReset

Walking-break reminders that check you actually walked

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.walkreset](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.walkreset)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-23

WalkReset puts walking-break reminders on your Wear OS watch, and a break only counts after a real five-minute walk of at least 300 steps, checked with the watch step counter. Snooze, pause, skip or stop for today from your wrist, and see today's breaks plus a seven-day summary. Standalone, with no account, ads, location or heart-rate use. Free runs every 30 minutes from 08:00 to 22:00; Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase for other intervals and custom schedules.

# SoulType

More than a personality test: insights tailored to you

- **Site:** [soul-type.app](https://soul-type.app/)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-26

Discover your personality through seven assessments, clear reports, and an AI coach grounded in your results. Explore strengths, blind spots, and relationships. Available on iPhone.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### How long does a test take?

Three minutes for the shortest. The Express assessment is a three-minute read, and the longest one, Smart Analysis, has a hundred questions.

> From a three-minute Express read to a hundred-question omnibus

### Which iOS version do I need?

iOS 26.0 or later, according to the App Store listing.

> Minimum iOS: 26.0

### Can I use SoulType on an Android phone?

Only on iPhone and iPad. The App Store listing names those two devices, and the website describes the app as built for iPhone.

> Devices: iPhone iPad

### Can I use the app in languages other than English?

The app is available in English, Japanese, French, and both Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

> the app ships in English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese and French

### How many AI coach messages are included for free?

Free users are provided with 20 messages to use with the AI coach every week.

> 20 coach messages a week

### Which personality frameworks does the app use?

The app evaluates your personality using 16 Personality, Big Five, Jungian Eight Functions, Attachment Style, Five Love Languages, and the Dark Triad.

> Explore 16 Personality, Big Five, Jungian Eight Functions, Attachment Style, Five Love Languages, Dark Triad, and Smart Analysis.

### Does the AI coach work offline?

No. The AI features need an internet connection, while your journal notes are saved on the device.

> AI features need an internet connection.

### Can I delete my data?

Yes. Settings wipes this device and the synced copies in one tap, and deleting the account removes the account itself.

> Settings wipes this device and the synced copies.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't work on Android devices.

  > Start exploring your personality with SoulType on iPhone.

- It doesn't provide clinical mental health diagnoses.

  > SoulType is designed for self-reflection and personal exploration, not medical or psychological diagnosis.

- It doesn't offer AI coaching without an internet connection.

  > The coach is online-only: check that you have a connection and try again.

- It doesn't sync AI conversation history between devices.

  > Coach conversations are never synced, so they stay on the phone that wrote them.

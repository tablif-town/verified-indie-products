# Cuewake

Cuewake is a native Mac app for notes, checklists, links, and files—leave a cue now and find it when you’re back.

- **Site:** [cuewake.app](https://cuewake.app/)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-17

Ever come back to your Mac and forget what you were doing? Cuewake lets you leave yourself a quick note, checklist, link or file right where you're working: an app, a webpage, a Finder folder or a project. When you come back, a little ghost peeks out of the notch to remind you. Set reminders in plain words like "tomorrow at 9". Works fully offline. No account, no subscription, no AI.

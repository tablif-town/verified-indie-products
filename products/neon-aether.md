### How much will Neon Aether cost?

The planned price is US$5.99. Regional prices and taxes may differ at release.

> Planned price: US$5.99.

### When does Neon Aether come out?

The target launch date is February 10, 2027. It is a target and may change as development continues.

> February 10, 2027 is the current target launch date.

### Which platforms is it planned for?

Windows and macOS through Steam, iOS through the App Store and Android through Google Play.

> Planned for Windows and macOS via Steam, iOS via the App Store, and Android via Google Play.

### Is there a Steam page I can wishlist?

Not yet. A public Steam store page has not been confirmed, so do not rely on unofficial listings.

> A public Steam store page has not been confirmed.

### Will there be a demo before launch?

Yes. The developer says there will be several demos or playtests, with access through the waitlist.

> Yes, there will be multiple, and you can get access by joining the waitlist

### What do I get for joining the waitlist?

Guaranteed early access, a guaranteed launch discount and development updates. Discount details come closer to launch.

> You’ll receive Neon Aether updates, guaranteed early access, and a guaranteed launch discount.

### Can I suggest ideas for the game?

Yes. Waitlist members can submit ideas, and selected ones may be featured, though submitting does not guarantee selection.

> Selected ideas may be featured in Neon Aether; submission does not guarantee selection.

### What kind of combat does it have?

Close-range combat with punches, kicks, blade attacks, blocking and power moves.

> Face opponents using punches, kicks, blade attacks, blocking, and documented power moves.

### What is the story about?

You play Andrew, an ordinary human who finds a portal in his home and lands on Aethelgard, a planet lit by several suns.

> Andrew is an ordinary human from Earth.

### What does the game look like?

Retro-inspired 16-bit pixel art, seen from the side as you move through each level.

> with a retro-inspired 16-bit pixel-art direction

### Are the system requirements known yet?

No. System requirements, editions and regional pricing have not been confirmed.

> System requirements, editions, regional pricing, and purchase links have not been confirmed.

### How long does it take to finish Neon Aether?

Around 10 to 20 hours for one playthrough, according to the developer.

> One playthrough will take around 10-20 hours

### Which languages will the game support?

English plus German, Spanish, Hindi and French at launch.

> German, Spanish, Hindi, and French

### Can I play the PC version with a controller?

Yes. The developer confirmed controller support for the PC version.

> Q: Will the PC version support a game controller? A (founder): Yes

### If I buy it on Steam, do I also get the phone version?

No. Each store is a separate purchase, so buying on Steam does not unlock the iOS or Android version.

> Is one purchase per store, so buying on Steam does not unlock the iOS or Android version? A (founder): Yes

### Are there in-app purchases on mobile?

No. The developer confirms the US$5.99 price is the full game on mobile too, with no in-app purchases.

> Is $5.99 the full game on mobile too, with no in-app purchases? A (founder): Yes

### Will my save carry over between PC and phone?

Probably not at launch. The developer says cross-save may not make the first version but is being worked on.

> It may not be able to do it in the first version when it launches, but we are working towards that.

### Do the mobile versions support gamepads?

No. Controller support is planned for the PC version only, not for iOS or Android.

> Will the mobile versions support game controllers? A (founder): No

### What age rating is it aiming for?

13+. The developer plans an age rating because of the blade combat.

> Is an age rating planned, given the blade combat? A (founder): Yes, 13+

### Can I remap keys and buttons on PC?

Maybe not at first. Remapping may be missing from version 1, and the developer says it is working toward it.

> They may not be able to in version 1 but we're working towards making that experience for everyone

### Does the game have puzzles?

Yes, many. The type of puzzle changes with the part of the story you are in.

> There are many puzzles, the type of puzzles correlate to the story.

### How big is the waitlist launch discount?

Not known yet. The size of the discount will be announced on January 1, 2027.

> The waitlist launch discount will be announced on January 1, 2027

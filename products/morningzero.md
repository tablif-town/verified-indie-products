# MorningZero

Every email account you own, in one AI-sorted inbox.

- **Site:** [morningzero.com](https://morningzero.com/)
- **Topic:** [Work and office](../topics/work-and-office.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-22

Stop checking inbox after inbox. MorningZero brings all your email into one place, shows you what needs you today, and helps you clear the rest. Connect Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, Fastmail, Zoho and more. AI sorts every email, drafts replies in your tone, and answers questions about your mail with the emails it used. Nothing sends until you press Send. On web, Mac, Windows, Linux and Android. 14 days free

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Is there an iPhone app?

No. It runs on the web, macOS, Windows, Linux and Android, and on an iPhone you use the web app in Safari.

> No iPhone app yet. MorningZero runs on the web, macOS, Windows, Linux and Android, and on an iPhone you can use the web app in Safari.

### Can I connect a Proton Mail account?

No. Proton and self-hosted mail servers are not supported, because Proton Bridge only runs on your own machine.

> It doesn't connect Proton, or a mail server you run yourself.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Does the yearly plan renew automatically?

No. Plans are a single yearly payment and there is no automatic renewal.

> لا يوجد تجديد تلقائي

### Can I use it for a site in Arabic?

Yes. The interface works in Arabic and English, for any market.

> عربي وإنجليزي، لأي سوق

### Does MIZAN show estimated search volumes or competitor traffic?

No. It does not show estimated search volumes, guessed competitor visits or rankings it has not measured.

> لا نعرض أحجام بحث تقديرية، ولا زيارات منافسين مخمنة، ولا سجلات ترتيب لم نقسها.

### Does MIZAN watch my site for SEO changes?

Yes. It takes periodic snapshots, compares them and alerts you in the app when titles, indexability, robots.txt or broken links change.

> نأخذ لقطات دورية ونقارنها، وننبهك داخل التطبيق عند تغير العناوين أو قابلية الفهرسة أو robots.txt أو الروابط المعطلة.

### Can MIZAN tell search engines about updated pages?

Yes. It supports IndexNow, which notifies participating search engines about URL changes quickly.

> نبّه محركات البحث الداعمة بتغييرات URLs بسرعة.

### How does the Google Search Console connection work?

Through OAuth: you sign in with Google, pick the property that matches your site, and the tokens are stored encrypted. You then see clicks, impressions, CTR and average position over 7, 28 or 90 days, kept apart from the crawler's own data.

> ربط OAuth آمن مع حفظ الرموز مشفرة

### Does the annual plan renew automatically?

No. Each plan is a single yearly payment with no automatic renewal, starting at $27 a year for up to 5 sites and 500 pages per scan.

> استخدم وسيلة الدفع المتاحة لك عبر صفحة الدفع الآمنة، دون تجديد تلقائي.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't show competitor traffic, backlinks or rankings unless a trusted data provider backs them; competitor analysis uses public pages only.

  > لا نعرض زيارات أو روابط خلفية أو ترتيبًا دون مزود بيانات موثوق.

- It doesn't compare you against more than five competitors at a time.

  > قارن موقعك بحتى خمسة منافسين

- It doesn't always show search volume for a keyword; metrics appear only when reliable data exists.

  > نعرض مقاييس البحث عندما تتوفر بيانات موثوقة

- It doesn't give unlimited free scans; the free scan is the first one for each domain.

  > فحص أول مجاني من ميزان سيو لكل نطاق. بلا بطاقة ائتمان.

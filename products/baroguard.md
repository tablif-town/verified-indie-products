# BaroGuard

Pressure trends, altitude and climb rate on Wear OS

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.baroguard](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.baroguard)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-21

BaroGuard turns a supported Wear OS watch into a standalone barometer. Check live pressure, local trends, approximate pressure-derived altitude and foreground-only climb/descent rate, with history, alerts, Tile and complication access.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### How much does BaroGuard cost?

The Google Play listing shows a price of 1.49 USD.

> Price: 1.49 USD

### How long does BaroGuard keep pressure history?

You can keep 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days of history on the watch.

> Keep 24-hour, 7-day, or 30-day history on your watch.

### Can I export my pressure data?

Yes. You can export the retained history as a CSV file when you choose.

> Export retained pressure history as CSV when you choose.

### Does the app use my location or an account?

No. There is no account, no location access, no internet access and no cloud sync, and readings and settings stay on the watch.

> Pressure readings, settings, history, calibration, and alert records stay on your watch.

### Can I correct the reading if it seems off?

Yes. You can calibrate against a trusted pressure reference or apply a quick offset.

> Calibrate against a trusted pressure reference or make quick offset adjustments.

### Are the pressure alerts weather warnings?

No. They tell you about pressure changes your watch measured, using the threshold, window, direction and cooldown you set. They are not forecasts or storm warnings.

> They are not weather forecasts, storm warnings, or emergency alerts.

### Will the Tile run down my battery?

It isn't designed to. The Tile and complications show cached readings and never start continuous background pressure monitoring.

> The Tile and complications use cached local readings and never start continuous background pressure monitoring.

### Does BaroGuard record pressure while the app is closed?

No. Live pressure, history and alerts update while BaroGuard is open, and the Tile and complications show the last cached reading.

> Live pressure, history, alerts, and vertical-speed sessions update while BaroGuard is open.

### Which pressure units can I use?

Three: hPa/mbar, inHg and mmHg. Pick the one your local weather service uses and comparing numbers gets easier.

> View live atmospheric pressure in hPa/mbar, inHg, or mmHg.

### Do I need my phone nearby?

No. BaroGuard runs on the watch itself and has no phone companion.

> Run the app directly on your watch with no phone companion required.

### Will BaroGuard work on any Wear OS watch?

No. Your watch needs a built-in barometer, so check its spec sheet before you buy.

> A Wear OS watch with a built-in barometer is required.

### Does the Tile show live pressure?

No. The Tile shows the latest cached reading. Tap it to open BaroGuard for live pressure.

> Add the latest cached reading to a Wear OS Tile or supported watch-face complication for quick access between checks.

### Are my climb and descent sessions saved?

No. Vertical-speed session results are shown while you use them but are not kept as permanent workout history.

> Session results are not saved as permanent workout history.

### What happens if I leave the app during a climb session?

The session stops. Vertical-speed sessions only run while BaroGuard stays in the foreground.

> Vertical-speed sessions stop when BaroGuard leaves the foreground or pressure readings pause.

### Which climb rates does it show?

Current, 5-second and 30-second rates, in m/s or ft/min.

> See current, 5-second, and 30-second rates in m/s or ft/min.

### Can I set my altitude to a known value?

Yes. You can set your current position as zero or enter a known reference altitude.

> Set your current position as zero or enter a known reference altitude.

### Can I choose when a pressure alert fires?

Yes. You pick the threshold, the trend window, the direction and a cooldown.

> Choose the threshold, trend window, direction, and cooldown.

### Can I use it for paragliding or flying?

No. BaroGuard is not a certified variometer, professional altimeter or aviation instrument.

> professional altimeter, certified variometer, or aviation instrument

### What can I put on a watch face complication?

Pressure, trend, delta, a gauge-style view or estimated altitude, on supported complication slots.

> Add pressure, trend, delta, gauge-style, or estimated-altitude data to supported complication slots.

### Does BaroGuard send any data to a server?

No. There is no backend or cloud sync, and readings, settings and history stay on the watch.

> Pressure readings, settings, history, calibration, and alert records stay on your watch.

### Does BaroGuard need an internet connection?

No. It reads the watch's own barometer and has no internet access.

> No internet access

### Can I see whether pressure is rising or falling?

Yes. It shows rising, falling or steady, or says it is still collecting enough data.

> See whether pressure is rising, falling, steady, or still collecting enough data.

### Is there a pressure chart?

Yes. You get a local pressure chart and can compare changes across the supported time windows.

> Review a local pressure chart and compare changes across supported time windows.

### Can a Tile show estimated altitude?

Yes. One of the Tile layouts shows estimated altitude; others show cached pressure, trend context or mini graphs.

> Use selectable Tile layouts for cached pressure, trend context, mini graphs, or estimated altitude.

### What do I see at the end of a climb session?

Relative altitude, duration, total ascent and descent, maximum climb and descent rates, and a small altitude graph.

> Review relative altitude, duration, total ascent and descent, maximum climb and descent rates, and a compact altitude graph.

### How accurate are the readings?

They are approximate. Results depend on the watch hardware, calibration, altitude changes, weather and the sensor itself.

> BaroGuard's readings, trends, alerts, estimated altitude, and vertical speed are approximate.

### Who makes BaroGuard?

It is published on Google Play by the developer Quazmoz.

> Developer: Quazmoz

### Does BaroGuard work on an Apple Watch or a watch that doesn't run Wear OS?

No. It needs a Wear OS watch, and that watch also has to have a built-in barometer.

> A Wear OS watch with a built-in barometer is required.

### Does BaroGuard show ads or collect analytics?

No. There are no ads and no analytics in the app.

> No ads • No analytics

### What does "still collecting enough data" mean on the trend?

Right after you start, the app may not have enough readings to call a direction yet. It tells you that instead of guessing rising, falling or steady.

> See whether pressure is rising, falling, steady, or still collecting enough data.

### Is BaroGuard a weather forecast app?

No. It shows the pressure your watch measures and how it has moved. It does not predict the weather.

> BaroGuard is not a certified weather station, weather forecast app, storm warning service, emergency alert service, medical tool, professional altimeter, certified variometer, or aviation instrument.

### What does BaroGuard show besides pressure?

Estimated altitude and vertical-speed sessions for climbs and descents. All of it comes from the watch's barometer.

> BaroGuard uses your watch’s built-in barometer for live readings, local trends, estimated altitude, and foreground-only vertical-speed sessions.

### What happens when I tap the Tile or complication?

It opens BaroGuard, so you can go from the cached number to live readings in one tap.

> Tap the Tile or complication to open BaroGuard.

### Does the altitude come from GPS?

No. Altitude is worked out from pressure and your local calibration, so treat it as approximate.

> View approximate pressure-derived altitude using local calibration.

### Can I use BaroGuard to track pressure for health reasons, like headaches?

Not as a medical tool. You can watch pressure change, but the app is not made or certified for medical use.

> BaroGuard is not a certified weather station, weather forecast app, storm warning service, emergency alert service, medical tool, professional altimeter, certified variometer, or aviation instrument.

### Are there in-app purchases or a subscription?

No. The developer confirms BaroGuard is a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases.

> Is BaroGuard a one-time 1.49 USD purchase with no in-app purchases?

### Are pressure alerts switched on after I install it?

No. Alerts are off by default, so nothing buzzes until you turn them on and pick your settings.

> Default threshold 3 hPa, 3-hour trend window, 3-hour cooldown, alerts off by default.

### How small can I set the alert threshold?

Down to 0.5 hPa. There are presets of 1, 2, 3 and 5 hPa, and a custom value from 0.5 to 10 hPa in steps of 0.5.

> Presets are 1/2/3/5 hPa, custom 0.5–10 hPa in 0.5 steps.

### How long does BaroGuard wait before alerting again?

Three hours by default, according to the developer. The cooldown is one of the settings, so one slow pressure drop can give you one buzz instead of several.

> Default threshold 3 hPa, 3-hour trend window, 3-hour cooldown, alerts off by default.

### What columns are in the CSV export?

The stored uncalibrated pressure, the calibrated pressure with your offset applied, and the calibration offset in effect when you exported, all in hPa.

> the CSV has filtered\_pressure\_hpa (stored, uncalibrated), calibrated\_pressure\_hpa (offset applied)

### How does exported data leave my watch?

Only when you start an export yourself and pick a system share target. Nothing is sent anywhere on its own.

> CSV export occurs only when you start it and choose a system share target.

### Does calibrating change the readings already stored?

No. The stored values stay uncalibrated and the offset is applied on top, which is why the CSV carries both versions plus the offset.

> filtered\_pressure\_hpa (stored, uncalibrated)

### Can I see how much pressure changed over a few hours?

Yes. The chart compares changes across time windows, and a watch-face complication can show the pressure delta.

> Add pressure, trend, delta, gauge-style, or estimated-altitude data to supported complication slots.

- It doesn't work on watches without a built-in barometer.

  > A Wear OS watch with a built-in barometer is required.

- It doesn't record pressure in the background; readings and history update while the app is open.

  > Live pressure, history, alerts, and vertical-speed sessions update while BaroGuard is open.

- It doesn't save vertical-speed sessions as workout history.

  > Session results are not saved as permanent workout history.

- It isn't a weather forecast, a storm warning service or a certified altimeter.

  > BaroGuard is not a certified weather station, weather forecast app, storm warning service

# Workout Interval HIIT Timer

A Wear OS interval timer you feel instead of watch

- **Site:** [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.intervaltimer](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quazmoz.intervaltimer)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-23

A standalone Wear OS timer for HIIT, Tabata, AMRAP, EMOM, boxing rounds and plain work/rest intervals. Different haptic cues mark work, rest, the countdown and the finish, so you don't need to look at the screen mid-round. No phone or account needed. A Tile starts your last preset and a complication shows timer status. Free includes the main timers and 1 saved preset; Pro is a one-time purchase for custom circuits and up to 20 presets.

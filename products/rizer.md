# RIZER

Sri Lanka's 1st Sustainable Fashion & rPET Fabric Brand

- **Site:** [rizer.lk](https://rizer.lk/)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-21

RIZER is Sri Lanka's pioneer in sustainable fashion, transforming post-consumer PET plastic bottles into high-performance rPET apparel and fabrics. We manufacture custom corporate T-shirts, event apparel, and uniforms with zero compromise on comfort, quality, or look. We offer worldwide delivery alongside our island-wide service in Sri Lanka.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### What fabrics can I choose for custom apparel?

Options include 100% polyester, recycled polyester and cotton blends.

> Fabrics: 100% Polyester, Recycled Polyester, Cotton blends.

### How do I contact RIZER about an order?

You can reach them through WhatsApp for questions and orders.

> Contact us easily through WhatsApp for quick responses to all your inquiries and orders.

### Does RIZER sell single items to individual shoppers?

No. RIZER works with customers who need coordinated apparel, not one-off retail pieces.

> RIZER works with customers who need coordinated apparel rather than one-off retail pieces.

### Is there a minimum order quantity?

No fixed minimum. The founder told us RIZER takes orders of any quantity.

> any quantity

### Can I get the shirts in my brand colour?

Yes. You can pick any colour and any size, and choose a fabric weight between 120 and 250 GSM.

> Choose any color, any size, and select thickness from 120-250 GSM.

### Which logo methods can they use?

The catalogue lists sublimation, hi-build, embroidery, heat transfer and DTF. Hi-build gives a raised print, and sublimation is common for all-over jersey designs.

> Logo Type All Sublimation Hibuild Embroidery Heat transfer DTF

### Will they help with the artwork and sizes?

Yes. Branding support covers colour selection, artwork guidance and size planning for the whole bulk order.

> Heat transfer, Hi-build, embroidery, color selection, artwork guidance, and size planning for bulk orders.

### Are the clothes really made from plastic bottles?

The recycled range is. PET plastic from bottles is turned into textile fibre and then into fabric. Check the fabric for your garment, because the catalogue also carries cotton and blends.

> Advanced recycling technology converts PET plastic into premium, sustainable textile fibers.

### Do they deliver outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. The Product Hunt launch post says RIZER delivers worldwide as well as islandwide in Sri Lanka.

> We offer worldwide delivery alongside our island-wide service in Sri Lanka.

### Can they make jerseys for a school or sports club?

Yes. Schools, universities and sports clubs are one of the main groups RIZER makes teamwear for, and the catalogue includes school and sports jerseys.

> Schools, universities, and sports clubs requiring durable, stylish, and environmentally responsible teamwear.

### Can I buy just the fabric?

Yes. Fabric made from recycled PET bottles is one of the product lines, next to the finished garments.

> Corporate T-Shirts, Promotional T-Shirts, Shirts, Sports Wear, Uniforms, Bottoms, Shorts, Skinnies, Caps, Ties, and Fabric made from recycled PET bottles.

### Which fabric weights can I choose?

Custom orders range from 120 to 250 GSM, so you can go lighter for event shirts or heavier for uniforms.

> select thickness from 120-250 GSM

### Where is RIZER based?

In Panadura, Sri Lanka, with a second location in Colombo 08.

> 70, Awariyawatta, Alubomulla, Panadura, Sri Lanka

### Can I visit before ordering?

Yes, but contact the team first to confirm availability and directions.

> Please contact the team before visiting to confirm availability and directions.

### What should I send to get a quote?

The garment type, estimated quantity, size breakdown, colours, logo or artwork, material needs and delivery location.

> For a useful quote, share your garment type, estimated quantity, size breakdown, preferred colours, logo or artwork, material requirements and delivery location.

### Can I order a shirt I saw in their client portfolio?

No. Client project pages are examples, not stock. RIZER can make a similar item with your own artwork.

> It is not a stock listing or an indication that the client-branded design is available for resale.

### How long does their recycled clothing take to break down?

RIZER says its clothing degrades in 15 to 20 years, compared with over 1000 years for a plastic bottle.

> fashion that degrades in 15-20 years instead of 1000+

### Do they make caps and ties too?

Yes. The range includes caps, ties, shorts, bottoms and fabric, besides shirts and uniforms.

> Corporate T-Shirts, Promotional T-Shirts, Shirts, Sports Wear, Uniforms, Bottoms, Shorts, Skinnies, Caps, Ties, and Fabric made from recycled PET bottles.

### Who have they worked with?

Companies, schools and organizations in Sri Lanka. Names listed on the site include Sri Lanka Airlines and Jetwing Travels.

> Collaborating with leading Sri Lankan companies to drive sustainable fashion forward.

### Do they make women's cuts, not just unisex shirts?

Yes. The catalogue includes women's pieces such as a boxy crop T-shirt and a modern uniform shirt.

> Custom Women's Modern Uniform Shirt

### Is there a stretch fabric option?

Yes. The fabric filter includes blends with 5% spandex, in both cotton and recycled polyester.

> 95% Recycle Polyester and 5% Spandex

### Can they make long-sleeve office shirts?

Yes. The range includes custom men's corporate shirts in both short and long sleeves.

> Custom Men's Corporate long-Sleeve Shirt

### Do they make shorts and bottoms for teams?

Yes. There are custom athletic bottoms and sports shorts for men and women.

> Custom Athletic Bottom

### Do they work with hotels and resorts?

Yes. The about page names hospitality and resort teams among the customers it supplies.

> hospitality and resort teams

### Can RIZER deliver anywhere in Sri Lanka?

Yes. They deliver island-wide in Sri Lanka as well as abroad.

> We offer worldwide delivery alongside our island-wide service in Sri Lanka.

### Is there a size guide?

Yes. The website links to a size guide you can use before sending your size breakdown.

> Size Guide

### Are prices listed on the website?

No. RIZER reviews your specifications first and then confirms suitable options and pricing.

> RIZER can review the specifications and confirm suitable options and pricing.

### Do I need permission for the logo I send?

Yes. Send artwork and branding you are authorised to use.

> provide artwork and branding you are authorized to use

### Are the fabric details in the portfolio fixed for my order?

No. Material and weight on a portfolio page describe that project only, and RIZER confirms specifications for any new order.

> These details refer to the pictured project only; RIZER will confirm specifications for any new order.

### Do they make custom T-shirts for events?

Yes. Their portfolio includes event T-shirts such as Coast to Coast 2026 and Raid Amazones.

> Event T-Shirt - Coast to Coast 2026

### Do they make corporate polo shirts with a company logo?

Yes. Corporate polos are a regular product, and past projects include a polo made for HNB.

> HNB Corporate Polo T-Shirt

### Can they work to a fixed date for an event?

Ask when you request a quote. Include the delivery location and the date you need the order, and RIZER confirms availability from that.

> Include the delivery location and requested timing so availability can be confirmed.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't make every garment from recycled plastic. The catalogue also lists 100% cotton and cotton and polyester blends.

  > 65% Cotton and 35% Polyester

- It isn't set up for one-off retail purchases. Orders are planned as matching apparel for a company, team or event.

  > RIZER works with customers who need coordinated apparel rather than one-off retail pieces.

# Vyne

A network for founders where your SI Avatar meets theirs.

- **Site:** [vyne.si](https://vyne.si/)
- **Topic:** [Marketing and sales](../topics/marketing-and-sales.md)

Vyne is a network for founders where your SI Avatar meets theirs first. Built from the memory you already keep in ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or Gemini, plus what you are building and what you need, it holds out for a reason that is good for both of you. You only hear who is worth meeting, and why.

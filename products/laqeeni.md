## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Which professional crafts are eligible for registration?

The directory supports over 35 crafts. These include plumbing, electricity, air conditioning, furniture moving, carpentry, car maintenance, and sewing.

> تكييف، سباكة، كهرباء، نقل عفش، دهان، نجارة، ورش سيارات، خياطة وغيرها. أكثر من ٣٥ حرفة.

### How does the platform protect the privacy of my uploaded documents?

Documents are stored in a private Google account accessible only to the team. They are kept in a private folder, never published, and can be deleted within 30 days upon request.

> في حساب لاقيني الخاص على Google، ولا يشوفها إلا فريق لاقيني. صور المستندات في مجلد خاص ولا تُنشر أبدًا.

### Does it cost anything to find someone on Laqeeni?

No. You browse the directory for free and message the business yourself, with no commission added.

> إحنا نتحقق منهم، وأنت تكلمهم مباشرة على الواتساب. مجاناً وبدون عمولة.

### Do I pay through Laqeeni?

No. Laqeeni does not take part in the deal or receive any money. You agree on the work, the price and the payment directly with the business.

> لا نكون طرفاً في أي اتفاق ولا نضمنه ولا نستلم أي مبلغ

### What does Laqeeni check about a business?

The business's mobile number at sign-up, plus its ID and any license or certificate it sends. Whatever is verified shows as a badge on its profile.

> نتحقق من رقم جوال النشاط عند التسجيل، ومن هويته وأي رخصة أو شهادة إذا أرسلها لنا، واللي نتحقق منه يظهر كشارة في ملفه.

### What can I look at before I message someone?

Their photos, prices and trust badges are on the listing, so you can compare before you reach out.

> شوف الصور والأسعار وشارات الثقة.

### Which cities can I filter by?

Riyadh, Jeddah, the Eastern Province, Makkah and Taif appear in the city filter.

> الرياض جدة الشرقية مكة المكرمة الطائف

### My city isn't listed. What can I do?

Tell the team on WhatsApp. The site asks people in missing cities to report them.

> مدينتك مو موجودة للحين؟ بلّغني على الواتساب

### Is it only for craftsmen?

No. It also lists home food and sweets, events, interior design and personal trainers.

> أكل بيت وحلويات، مناسبات، تصميم داخلي، مدربين وحرفيين

### Is there a subscription for businesses that list?

No. Registration is free, with no commission and no subscription.

> التسجيل مجاني، بدون عمولة وبدون اشتراك.

### Who runs Laqeeni?

Laqeeni Solutions Establishment, a registered business in Riyadh. Its commercial registration number is shown in the site footer.

> مؤسسة لاقيني سولوشنز · سجل تجاري ٧٠٥١٢٤٣٤٨٨ · الرياض، المملكة العربية السعودية

### Can I find a female personal trainer?

Yes. The trainers section has a separate list of women trainers who work at your home, at a gym or online.

> مدربات نساء بالبيت، النادي أو أونلاين

### What do the trust badges on a profile mean?

Each badge shows something Laqeeni checked. There are four: verified ID, commercial registration, licence and real photos of past work.

> هوية موثّقة سجل تجاري رخصة صور شغل حقيقية

### How many businesses are listed?

More than 520 local businesses, according to the home page, with new ones joining regularly.

> أكثر من ٥٢٠ نشاط محلي

### How do I report a problem with a listing?

Through WhatsApp. The site footer has a WhatsApp link for reporting problems.

> للإبلاغ عن مشكلة: واتساب

### Can I use the site in English?

Yes. Laqeeni has an Arabic and an English version, and you can switch between them from the menu.

> العربية English

### Is there anything for business owners who read Urdu, Bengali or Hindi?

Yes. The site has pages for business owners in Urdu, Bengali and Hindi, linked at the bottom of every page.

> لأصحاب الأنشطة: اردو · বাংলা · हिन्दी

### Does Laqeeni guarantee the quality of the work?

No. Laqeeni tries to check the information businesses give, but it is not responsible for their service or how they deal with you.

> نبذل جهدنا للتحقق من معلومات الأنشطة المدرجة، لكننا غير مسؤولين عن خدماتهم أو تعاملهم.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't offer services to customers outside of Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Khobar, and Dhahran yet.

  > لاقيني متوفر للعملاء الحين في الرياض وجدة والدمام والخبر والظهران، ونفتح مدن ثانية قريب.

- It doesn't process payments or handle price negotiations between the customer and the craftsman.

  > العميل يكلمك مباشرة على الواتساب أو الاتصال. تتفقون على السعر بينكم

- It doesn't provide an automated booking or scheduling system within the platform.

  > العميل يكلمك مباشرة على الواتساب أو الاتصال.

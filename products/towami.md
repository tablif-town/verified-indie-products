# Towami

- **Site:** [towami.com](https://towami.com)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)

Towami started as a classifieds board. 2.0 adds a full online store on your own domain, white-label and set up automatically. Publish any product to your country's marketplace in one click. Flat monthly fee, 0% commission, so you keep 100% of every sale. Stores are built for 100/100 PageSpeed, with SEO tools, 301 redirects, multi-warehouse inventory, card payments and promo codes. Live in 9 markets: USA, Mexico and Central America. Free plan available.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Can I add informational pages to my store?

Yes. The Free plan allows up to 5 informational pages, and higher plans allow more.

> Up to 5 informational pages.

### Can I manage multiple warehouses?

Yes. The Business plan adds support for up to 2 warehouses, while higher plans include more.

> Up to 2 warehouses

### Can my online store support multiple languages?

Yes. The Start plan and higher include the ability to use multiple languages.

> Multilingual storefront

### Can I list services or real estate instead of physical products?

Yes. The platform supports listings for vehicles, real estate, and services alongside standard products. Each plan has specific limits for these categories.

> Up to 300 products. Vehicles, real estate and services: 20 each.

### How fast do the store pages load?

The platform is designed for high performance on mobile and desktop. Catalog pages are typically generated in 5 to 10 milliseconds.

> Catalog pages typically generated in 5-10 milliseconds

### What SEO features are included with the store?

Towami provides server-rendered pages and clean URLs to help with search rankings. It also includes built-in sitemaps, canonical links, and structured data.

> Sitemaps, canonical links and structured data built in

### Can my staff have their own logins?

Yes, from the Profi plan, with up to 10 staff accounts. Enterprise has no limit on staff accounts.

> Staff accounts (up to 10 people)

### Which countries can I sell in?

Nine markets for now: Belize, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua, Panama and the USA.

> Choose a country Belize Costa Rica El Salvador Guatemala Honduras Mexico Nicaragua Panama USA

### Can I import my products from a spreadsheet?

Yes, from the Business plan. It imports and exports products as Google XML or CSV files.

> Product import/export (Google XML, CSV)

### Can I offer discount codes?

Yes, from the Start plan, which adds promo codes for the cart.

> Promo codes for the cart

### Can I sell the same product in different sizes and colors?

Yes, even on Free, as simple variants without separate SKUs. Variants with their own SKU, price and stock come with the Business plan.

> Simple product variants (size, color) without SKU

### Can I remove Towami's branding from my store?

Yes, on the Profi plan and above, which include a white-label storefront.

> Everything from the Business plan White-label storefront

### What are the classifieds on Towami?

Towami also runs classifieds where private sellers list items. Buyers who come for those listings can find products from shops too, which brings more local traffic to stores.

> Buyers come looking for private listings and also find products from professional shops.

### Is there a free plan?

Yes. Stores start on the Free plan with up to 20 products, and you can upgrade when you need more.

> Stores start on Free (20 products).

### How much does a paid plan cost?

It depends on the country. Choose your country on the site to see prices, and Enterprise is priced on request.

> Prices differ depending on the country.

### Can I take card payments online?

Yes, from the Business plan up. Online payments are listed there, not on Free or Start.

> Online payments

### Can I use my own domain?

Yes. You can start on a Towami subdomain and connect your own custom domain later.

> You can start with a Towami subdomain and connect your own custom domain for your storefront.

### Do I need plugins for the basics?

No. The essential features are built in, and updates and compatibility are Towami's job.

> No third-party plugins required for essential features

### Can customers leave reviews on my store?

Yes, on the Business plan and above, which add reviews on the storefront.

> Reviews on the storefront

### Can I connect Towami to my CRM?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan, which includes API integrations for a CRM.

> API integrations (CRM)

### Which company runs Towami?

Gasketo LLC, based in Sheridan, Wyoming, in the United States.

> Company Gasketo LLC

### Which languages can the storefront use?

English and Spanish, according to the founder. Multilingual storefronts start on the Start plan.

> A (founder): English and Spanish

### Can I start a store without my own domain?

Yes. Every store can start on a Towami subdomain, and you can connect a custom domain later.

> You can start with a Towami subdomain and connect your own custom domain for your storefront.

### How many products can I add on each plan?

Free allows 20, Start 300, Business 1,000 and Profi 3,000. Enterprise has no product cap.

> Stores start on Free (20 products). Start includes 300 products, Business 1,000, Profi 3,000.

### Can I set up 301 redirects from my old store?

Yes, on the Start plan and above, along with search preview and Search Console.

> Advanced SEO: Search preview, 301 redirects, Search Console

### Can I give each product variant its own price and stock?

Yes, on Business and above, with product SKUs that have their own price and stock. The Free plan has simple variants without SKUs.

> Product SKUs with own price and stock

### Can I upgrade later as my catalog grows?

Yes. Plans are set by product limits, and you move up when you need more products.

> Upgrade when you need more products

### Does the Free plan include a shopping cart?

Yes. The Free plan has a shopping cart, a fast storefront and basic SEO.

> Shopping cart

### Can I publish to the marketplace on the Free plan?

Yes. Publishing to the regional marketplace is part of the Free plan.

> Publish to the marketplace

### Will my store work on phones?

Yes. Store layouts are mobile-friendly by default.

> Mobile-friendly layouts by default

### Do I need to code to change text or images?

No. You change store content without touching code.

> Change content without touching code

### Can I run a blog on my store?

Only on Profi and above. The shop blog is part of the Profi plan.

> Shop blog

### Who handles updates when something changes?

Towami does. Updates, performance and compatibility are the team's job, not yours.

> Updates, performance and compatibility are our responsibility

### Are the prices the same in every country?

No. Prices differ by country, so choose your country on the site to see them.

> Prices differ depending on the country.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't include online payments on the Free and Start plans. They start with Business.

  > Everything from the Start plan Online payments

- It doesn't offer storefront languages beyond English and Spanish, according to the founder.

  > English and Spanish

- It doesn't cover every country. The markets listed are Central American countries, Mexico and the USA.

  > Belize Costa Rica El Salvador Guatemala Honduras Mexico Nicaragua Panama USA

- It doesn't guarantee a 100/100 PageSpeed score. That is the engineering target, and your content affects it.

  > Final scores depend on the content you upload (photo size, third-party scripts)

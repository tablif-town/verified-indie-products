## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Does the app require a monthly subscription?

No, there are no monthly subscriptions or recurring fees. You pay once for your country's plan and top up minutes only when needed.

> No monthly bills, no contracts.

### Does live transcript generation require internet access?

Yes, live call transcription requires an active internet connection via mobile data or Wi-Fi.

> A live transcript needs mobile data or Wi-Fi.

### How are calls translated if the caller speaks another language?

The assistant prompts the caller in their language and translates their spoken words onto your screen in real time using downloaded language packs.

> Every word the caller speaks is transcribed in real time and translated into your chosen language.

### Do I need a new SIM card or phone number?

No. It works with the number you already have, with no SIM change.

> Works with your existing number, with no SIM change

### Do calls from my contacts use up minutes?

No. Contacts ring straight through and never touch your minutes.

> Contacts ring straight through and never use minutes

### Will my voicemail still work?

Yes. Voicemail still takes the calls you miss. Only the setting for calls that arrive while your line is busy changes.

> Your voicemail still catches the calls you miss

## What it does not do

- It doesn't support iOS devices.

  > Android call screening and spam call blocking for any phone.

- It doesn't screen calls without an active mobile data or Wi-Fi connection.

  > A live transcript needs mobile data or Wi-Fi.

- It doesn't forward calls when your phone line is not busy.

  > It only ever applies when your line is busy.

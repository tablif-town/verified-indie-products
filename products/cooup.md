# COOUP

Creator-powered commerce for Shopify brands

- **Site:** [cooup.co](https://cooup.co/)
- **Topic:** [Marketing and sales](../topics/marketing-and-sales.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-30

COOUP connects Shopify brands with creators who share products they love. Choose products, set creator rewards and customer discounts, and track referred sales, commissions and settlements in one place. Shopify remains the home for products, orders and checkout. Plans start at US$29/month with a 90-day free trial.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Does COOUP use Google Analytics on its website?

Yes, public website visits may be tracked using Google Analytics if allowed by the user. Opting out does not impact account functionality or creator sale attribution.

> If you allow it, Google Analytics helps us understand visits to our public website. Your choice does not affect your account or creator-sale attribution.

### How can users contact COOUP for customer support?

Support is available via email at support&&#35;64;cooup.co. COOUP aims to reply within one business day for general merchant or creator questions.

### How much does COOUP cost for Shopify brands?

Merchant plans start at US$29 per month with a 90-day subscription trial. Merchants also fund commission on eligible creator-attributed sales: 90% of that commission goes to the creator and 10% to COOUP.

> Plans start at US$29/month with a 90-day free trial.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't process customer checkouts or manage product inventory outside of Shopify.

  > Shopify remains the home for products, orders and checkout.

- It doesn't credit creators for general store orders that lack a valid referral link.

  > COOUP does not count unrelated store orders as creator-attributed sales.

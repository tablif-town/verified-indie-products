# FlowpyDisk

AI Workspaces for your teams

- **Site:** [flowpydisk.com](https://flowpydisk.com/)
- **Topic:** [AI and agents](../topics/ai-and-agents.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-20

FlowpyDisk is an AI productivity suite: Drive, business email, team chat, meetings, notes, tables, tasks and calendar in one workspace. We built the entire suite on a pre-indexed semantic and graph data layer. Your workspace is indexed once into a knowledge graph and vector search, so you and your AI agents get instant, connected context instead of rebuilding it every time. Plug in any agent or automation via MCP and API. No per-seat pricing. Start free.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't allow accounts to exceed 25 total workspaces even on Pro plans.

  > Workspaces 2 25 25

- It doesn't permit AI agents to perform permanent file deletions or public sharing without human approval.

  > Publishing, sharing and permanent deletes stay with people.

# InvoiceKit by RoohSites

Professional invoices. No signup. No subscription.

- **Site:** [invoice.roohsites.com](https://invoice.roohsites.com/)
- **Topic:** [Work and office](../topics/work-and-office.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-01

Make professional invoices in minutes with a free, browser-based invoice generator. Add your business details, customize line items, calculate taxes and discounts, and download a PDF — without creating an account or paying a subscription. Supports multiple currencies, GST and VAT, with invoice data kept on your device.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### How do users send completed invoices to clients?

Once built, the invoice can be downloaded as a PDF file to your device and attached to an email.

> Click Download PDF, save it, and attach it to an email.

### Can client information be saved for repeat invoicing?

You can save client details locally in your browser to quickly generate invoices for repeat clients.

> Add your logo, save clients for next time, reopen past invoices from your history

### Does InvoiceKit offer customizable layout templates?

Users can select from Classic, Modern, and Minimal template layouts when creating an invoice.

> choose between the Classic, Modern and Minimal designs, all free.

### Is InvoiceKit really free?

Yes. It is free to use with no signup and no subscription.

> No signup, no subscription, and nothing leaves your browser.

### Is my invoice data sent to a server?

No. The invoice is built in your browser and nothing leaves it.

> nothing leaves your browser

### Which currencies can I use?

Any currency, with a list that includes USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD, AUD, AED, JPY, BRL and many more.

> Any currency

### Do I have to sign up before making an invoice?

No. You fill in the form and download the PDF without an account or a subscription.

> No signup, no subscription, and nothing leaves your browser.

### Can I add GST or VAT, including CGST and SGST?

Yes. You set a tax name and rate, and a split option shows the tax in two halves, such as CGST and SGST.

> Split tax in half (e.g. CGST + SGST)

### Will my invoices show up on another computer?

No. Everything is stored in the browser on the device you used, so history and saved clients stay there.

> Everything is stored only in your browser on this device.

### What happens to my history if I clear my browser data?

It is lost. History lives only in the browser, so download and keep a copy of every PDF you send.

> Where is invoice history stored, in the browser only, and is it lost if the browser data is cleared?

### Can I put my logo on the invoice?

Yes. Adding a logo is free, like every other feature.

> Every feature is free today: logo, saved clients, history and all designs.

### Can I make a proforma invoice or a bill of supply?

Yes. The document title can be set to Invoice, Tax Invoice, Bill of Supply or Proforma Invoice, or left on Auto.

> Bill of Supply

### Can I reuse an old invoice for a repeat job?

Yes. Reopen it from your history and duplicate it in one click, then change the dates and items.

> reopen past invoices from your history, duplicate an invoice in one click

### Are there ads inside the tool?

No. There are no ads in the invoice tool, and the team says it never sells data because it never has it.

> There are no ads in the tool, and we never sell data, partly because we never have it.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't provide tax or legal advice.

  > InvoiceKit doesn't give tax advice; check local invoicing rules with an accountant.

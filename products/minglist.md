# MingList

Free local classifieds. Your email stays private.

- **Site:** [minglist.top](https://minglist.top/)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-27

MingList is a free, privacy-first alternative to platforms like Craigslist or Gumtree. Built for users who value data sovereignty, it features an automatic anonymous email relay system to hide your real identity from scammers and spammers. No public profiles, no data tracking, and zero friction. Browse and post freely without compromising your personal privacy.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Which cities is MingList in?

24 cities across the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia and Singapore, from San Francisco and New York City to Toronto, London, Sydney and Singapore.

> Free local classifieds in 24 cities across five countries.

### Why do I have to sign in with Google to post?

So MingList knows where to forward replies. It also means there are no passwords on the site to steal, and it filters out most spam.

> It keeps the site free of passwords to steal and filters out most spam, without creating another account to manage.

### Is it free to post an ad?

Yes. Posting, browsing, replying and renewing ads are all free, and there are no promoted slots to buy.

> Yes. Posting, browsing, replying and renewing ads are all free.

### What happens to the relay address when I delete a listing?

Deleting an ad disables the email relay immediately. Once a listing is removed, no further buyer replies will reach your inbox.

> Deletion stops the relay address immediately.

### How are classified listings ordered on the platform?

Classified ads are ordered strictly by location and publication time. The site does not use algorithmic feeds or paid promotions to rank listings.

> Listings are sorted by place and time, not by what keeps you scrolling.

### Can I reply to buyer inquiries directly from my standard email client?

Yes, sellers reply using their regular email client. MingList routes the response back through the relay address so the buyer never sees your personal email.

> You reply from your own email client. MingList sends it back through the relay.

### Will buyers see my email address?

No. Replies go through an anonymous email relay and land in your real inbox, but nobody sees your address.

> They never see your email address, and neither does anyone else reading the page.

### Is there a public profile with my name?

No. There is no public profile page, so your listing history does not follow you around.

> There's no public profile page with your name on it

### Do I need an account to reply to an ad?

No. Browsing and replying never need an account. Only posting uses a one-click Google sign-in.

> Browsing and replying never need an account.

### Does MingList check listings or handle payments?

No to both. It doesn't verify users or inspect listings, and you and the buyer settle payment in person.

> We don't verify users or inspect listings, and MingList never handles payments

### Can I sell digital goods or online courses?

No. Only physical items handed over in person, in the same city, are in scope.

> Digital goods, online courses, remote work, drop-shipping, and posts that exist to send people to an off-site checkout are out of scope

### Can my photos reveal where I live?

No. Photos are re-encoded in your browser before upload, which removes the location and camera data phones add.

> They are re-encoded in your browser before upload, which strips the location and camera data that phone photos usually carry.

### How long does an ad stay up?

30 days unless you choose otherwise. When you post, you can set any run time up to 365 days.

> 30 days by default; you can ask for anything up to 365.

### Can I renew an ad that is about to run out?

Yes, free, from My ads. Renewing pushes the expiry date out and turns the relay back on if it had stopped.

> Renew extends the expiry date and switches the relay back on if it had stopped.

### How many photos can I add?

Up to 8 per ad, each under 2 MB.

> Up to 8, each under 2 MB.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't verify the identity of sellers or buyers on the platform.

  > We don't verify users.

- It doesn't process transactions or hold money between buyers and sellers.

  > We don't handle payments.

- It doesn't guarantee that an item will sell or that replies are genuine.

  > We don't guarantee outcomes.

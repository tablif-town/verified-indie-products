# Randomverse

Social, without expectations.

- **Site:** [randomverse-social.vercel.app](https://randomverse-social.vercel.app)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)

An algorithm-free social platform built for serendipitous content discovery.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Can I explore the platform at my own speed?

Yes. You can browse the content at your own pace and save things that interest you.

> Explore Randomverse at your own pace, save what catches your attention, and shape your own space without unnecessary friction.

### How can I help keep the community safe?

You can flag content that is not appropriate to help maintain a good experience for others.

> Report inappropriate content and help keep discovery enjoyable for everyone.

### Can I create a personal space on the platform?

Yes. You can create a profile to establish your own corner of the platform.

> Create a profile and make your own corner of Randomverse.

### Is the platform designed for viral content?

No. It focuses on curiosity and discovery rather than chasing likes or going viral.

> A place where curiosity matters more than popularity.

### Do I have to gain followers for people to see my posts?

No. Every post is part of a global feed, so anyone can see your content regardless of your follower count.

> Your posts become part of Randomverse's global discovery experience, giving people around the world a chance to come across what you share.

### Does the platform use my location to show me local posts?

No. The experience is not based on where you are located.

> Your experience isn't built around your personal profile, popularity, or location.

### Is there a way to keep posts I like?

Yes. You can save any post that interests you to look at it again later.

> Save anything that catches your attention and revisit it anytime.

### How do I discover new posts?

You simply open the platform and swipe to see a changing stream of content from around the world.

> Open Randomverse. Swipe. Discover something you didn't expect.

### Will I know what kind of content to expect next in the feed?

No. The platform is designed so that you do not know what comes next, making the unexpected part of the experience.

> You don't need to know what comes next. That's part of the experience.

### Is there a limit on how many posts I can save?

No. You can save as many posts as you like without any restrictions.

> No, users can save as many posts as they like without any limits.

### Where can I reach the people behind Randomverse?

The about page links to their LinkedIn, GitHub, X, Instagram and Mastodon accounts, and to an email address.

> LinkedIn GitHub X Instagram Mastodon Email

### Can people in other countries see what I post?

Yes. Posts go into one global stream, so someone far from where you live can come across yours.

> Your posts become part of Randomverse's global discovery experience, giving people around the world a chance to come across what you share.

### Is it only for people who post?

No. It is meant for both sides: people who just want to look around and see something unexpected, and creators who want their work to have a chance of being seen.

> users discover unexpected content from around the world and creators share their work with a chance of being seen

### Who looks at a post after I report it?

People do. Reports go to human moderators, who check the content and decide what action to take.

> Reports are reviewed by human moderators who assess the content and take appropriate action.

### Can I follow a creator whose posts I like?

Not at the moment. Posts reach you as you swipe, not through a list of people you follow, so save a post you want to see again.

> Not currently. Randomverse focuses on discovering content organically rather than following individual creators.

### Can I edit a post after I share it?

No. Editing isn't supported right now, so check the text and the photo or video before you post.

> Post editing is not currently supported.

### Is the site available in Arabic?

Yes, both the platform and about page have an Arabic version as well as the English one.

> Privacy العربية

### Will I see a post again after I report it?

No. It is hidden for you so you do not come across it again, while others can still see it until moderators decide.

> The reported post is hidden from the user who reported it to not encounter it again if they scroll back.

### Can I read posts written in another language?

Yes. The main languages for the UI are English and Arabic, and you can translate text posts into any other language you like.

> users can translate text posts to any other language of their liking

### Is there a mobile app?

Not yet in the stores. iOS and Android apps are in early testing and will be announced when they are released.

> They're currently at early stage testing, and will be announced when they're fully released to their respective stores.

### Can I search for a topic or tag?

No. Randomverse puts spontaneous discovery ahead of targeted search.

> Randomverse prioritizes spontaneous discovery over targeted searches.

### Can I delete a post I shared?

Yes. Creators can delete any post they shared, even though editing is not supported.

> Yes, content creators can delete any post they shared.

### Does Randomverse use an algorithm to rank posts?

No. It's an algorithm-free, privacy-first social platform.

> An algorithm-free, privacy-first social platform.

### Will the feed keep showing me the same posts?

Rarely. The feed is built to cut down on repeats so it stays fresh.

> Repeated posts are rare. Randomverse is designed to reduce repetition and give users a fresh discovery experience.

### Can anyone use the translate option?

Yes, everyone can use the translate post feature.

> The translation option is available to signed-in users.

### What numbers do creators see about their posts?

Overall views, shares, favorites and the countries where people saw the post, plus insights for each post.

> It shows views, shares, favorites, countries, and insights of individual posts.

### Is there a length or size limit for videos?

No. You can upload videos of any length and size.

> No, users can upload videos of any length and size.

### What kinds of posts can I share?

Photos, videos or text. There is no set format you have to fit.

> Share photos, videos, or text without pressure to perform, compete, or fit a particular format.

### What do I need an account for?

Posting, favorites, report inappropriate content, and customizing a profile. Browsing works without one.

> An account is only needed for features such as posting, favorites, and profile customization.

### Do I need an account just to look around?

No. You can browse Randomverse without signing up or making a profile.

> Explore Randomverse without signing up or creating a profile.

### Do I need to set anything up to get engagement insights?

No. Every creator with an account gets insights without extra setup.

> Are engagement insights available to every creator with an account, with no extra setup?

### Do I have to wait for the apps to try it?

No. You can try Randomverse now from its website; only the native iOS and Android apps are still in testing.

> Try Randomverse

### Can I join the iOS or Android beta?

No. The native apps are in early testing, and the team asks people to wait for the store release.

> People should wait for the store release.

### Will it always be free?

The existing core experience is intended to remain accessible for free. With Optional subscription-based features or services may be introduced as the platform grows.

> we may introduce optional subscription-based features or services as the platform evolves.

### Does Randomverse cost anything to use today?

No. It is completely free to use right now, for browsing and for posting.

> Randomverse is currently completely free to use

## What it does not do

- It doesn't let you edit a post after it is published.

  > Post editing is not currently supported.

- It doesn't let you follow individual creators.

  > Randomverse focuses on discovering content organically rather than following individual creators.

- It doesn't offer keyword or tag search.

  > Randomverse prioritizes spontaneous discovery over targeted searches.

- It doesn't have released iOS or Android apps yet.

  > They're currently at early stage testing

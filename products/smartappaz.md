# SmartApp.az

All-in-one platform for POS, inventory, and CRM

- **Site:** [smartapp.az/en](https://smartapp.az/en)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-16

SmartApp.az provides a centralized platform to manage business operations from orders to analytics. It brings together point-of-sale (POS) systems in one place.It also includes QR menus for digital ordering.It tracks inventory.It manages customer relationships (CRM) through the same interface. Businesses can streamline their daily workflows, monitor stock levels, and analyze performance data to run their operations more efficiently. SmartApp is a complete digital solutions package designed to streamline and automate business operations. Built with modern technology, SmartApp connects front-of-house and back-of-house workflows into a single intuitive platform. Key Features & Modules: • Real-time Order & Table Management with instant Kitchen Display System (KDS) integration • Automated Inventory & Stock Control with low-stock alerts and recipe-based deduction • Employee Management Panel, attendance tracking, and performance analytics • Interactive QR Menu for contactless table ordering • Full POS Hardware and Payment Integration (Cards, Crypto, Cash) • Multi-branch management with unified cloud analytics and visual revenue reports • Offline mode support ensuring uninterrupted daily operations Empower your team, elevate customer experience, and scale your business with SmartApp.

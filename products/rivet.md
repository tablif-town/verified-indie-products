# Rivet

AI coding agent that runs on your Android phone

- **Site:** [github.com/Kaiser0733/rivet](https://github.com/Kaiser0733/rivet)
- **Topic:** [AI and agents](../topics/ai-and-agents.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-30

Rivet is a chat-first AI coding agent built native for Android. Pick a project folder, pick a model, describe the change — Rivet reads and edits files and runs commands right on your phone. No laptop, no Termux setup. Bring your own key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter), or sign in with a ChatGPT plan. Switch models and reasoning levels mid-chat, and undo changes with checkpoints. Free and open source.

### Is Rivet on the Google Play Store?

No. You download the APK from the GitHub Releases page and open it, and Android asks for permission as it does for any app from outside the Play Store.

> Download the APK from Releases and open it.

### Can I use a ChatGPT subscription instead of an API key?

Yes. Besides bringing your own key, you can sign in with a ChatGPT plan.

> You can also sign in with a ChatGPT plan.

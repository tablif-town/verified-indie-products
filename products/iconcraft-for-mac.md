# IconCraft for Mac

Rename and re-icon any Mac app, reversibly

- **Site:** [iconcraft.co](https://iconcraft.co/)
- **Topic:** [Desktop and utilities](../topics/desktop-and-utilities.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-23

Give any Mac app a custom name and icon in Finder, the Dock and Launchpad, without changing how the app works. Apply Full Skin keeps your name and icon while the app runs, and the new Edit panel (0.14.0) does rename and full skin in one save. Everything is reversible. Now in English, 中文, 日本語 and 한국어, following your macOS language. Free and open source (MIT), signed and notarised, macOS 12.3+ on Apple Silicon and Intel. Made by Gavin Shipman.

# Particle Gateway

AI at work, with the client taken out

- **Site:** [gateway.particlelabs.ai](https://gateway.particlelabs.ai/)
- **Topic:** [Constraints and privacy](../topics/constraints-and-privacy.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-07

Use ChatGPT and Claude at work without handing them your clients. Particle Gateway masks names, National Insurance numbers, UTRs and bank details on your computer before a message is sent. Your team sees what they typed; the AI only ever sees placeholders.

# FrontendGeek

Curated preparation hub for frontend interviews

- **Site:** [frontendgeek.com](https://frontendgeek.com/)
- **Topic:** [Education](../topics/education.md)

FrontendGeek provides a structured preparation hub for developers looking to succeed in frontend interviews. It offers a curated collection of interview questions and resources tailored for both freshers and experienced engineers, organized specifically by interview rounds to streamline the study process.

## Longer reads

- **[How to prepare for every round of a frontend interview](https://answers.delivery/frontendgeek/frontend-interview-preparation-questions-and-resources)**  
  If you have a frontend interview coming up, you want one place that shows what each round covers and points to good material, not a pile of links.
- **[How to practice building UI components for a live interview](https://answers.delivery/frontendgeek/how-to-practice-for-a-frontend-machine-coding-round)**  
  You have a frontend machine coding round coming up and you need to build a working component, like a modal or an accordion, under a timer.

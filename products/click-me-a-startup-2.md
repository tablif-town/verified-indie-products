# Click me a Startup 2

Can you build a Startup in 365 days one click at a time?

- **Site:** [praveenax.itch.io/click-me-a-startup-2](https://praveenax.itch.io/click-me-a-startup-2)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-10

You have 365 in-game days to turn a solo coding gig into a software company. Write code, hire developers and AI agents, and ship projects to earn revenue. Grow too fast and tech debt and payroll can catch up with you. Play in your browser.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Is there a cost to play the game?

The game is free to download and play, but you can choose to pay a custom amount to support the developer's work.

> This game is free but the developer accepts your support by letting you pay what you think is fair for the game.

### Can I play the game without downloading it?

Yes, you can play the game directly in your web browser without needing to download the project files.

> Play in your browser.

### How do I earn money in the game?

You generate revenue by completing software projects and shipping them to the market using your team's output.

> Ship projects to earn money

## What it does not do

- It doesn't provide a way to continue playing once the 365-day timer expires.

  > You have just 365 in-game days to build something extraordinary.

- It doesn't allow you to start the game with any employees or initial funding.

  > Start as a solo developer with zero employees, zero revenue, and one simple skill: writing code.

- It doesn't include a multiplayer mode for competing against other players.

  > You have 365 in-game days to turn a solo coding gig into a software company.

# BookingMachine

Quote-first booking for local service businesses

- **Site:** [bookingmachine.io](https://bookingmachine.io/)
- **Topic:** [Work and office](../topics/work-and-office.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-10-30

A booking form for local service businesses, on your own website. Customers see the price as they pick options, book a time and can pay a deposit. After the job it asks for a photo or video review. Also works from a link, a QR code or a free website.

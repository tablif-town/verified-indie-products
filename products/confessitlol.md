# Confessit.lol

Anonymous college confessions, minus the sign-up

- **Site:** [confessit.lol](https://confessit.lol/)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-10

Every college has stories students won't share under their real names. Confessit.lol lets adults across Indian campuses post confessions, hostel stories, exam rants and placement experiences without signing up. Browse campus feeds, react and reply.

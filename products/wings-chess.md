# Wings Chess

Learn chess with an AI coach that explains every move

- **Site:** [wingschess.com](https://wingschess.com/)
- **Topic:** [Personal](../topics/personal.md)
- **Ownership verified:** 2026-11-09

Wings Chess is a free chess coach that explains every move in plain English. Stockfish finds the moves and the AI only explains what the engine has verified, so it never invents a line. - 92 lessons across 5 levels, from your first move to club level - Import your Lichess or Chess.com games and the mistakes you repeat become puzzles - 10 bots that play and blunder like real players from 400 to 2000 - Play a friend with one link, no download

# InviteVia

Modern, interactive digital invitations for any event

- **Site:** [inviteviaapp.com](https://inviteviaapp.com/)
- **Topic:** [Industry](../topics/industry.md)

InviteVia lets you design and share beautiful digital invitations for weddings, birthdays, and any celebration. Choose from elegant templates, add event details, and publish a single link you can share anywhere. Guests can RSVP in seconds without accounts, including plus-ones and meal preferences, while real-time dashboards track responses. The platform supports 19 languages and offers a simple free start with paid upgrades for more features.

## Quick answers

Each answer comes with the sentence it is based on, quoted from the product's own pages.

### Can I add music to my digital invitation?

Yes, you can include custom music on your invitation using MP3 files or Spotify. This option is available on the Premium plan.

> Custom music (MP3 + Spotify)

### How many languages can I create invitations in?

InviteVia allows you to create and share invitations in 19 different languages.

> Create and share invitations in 19 languages.

### How long does Premium last?

Premium is bought per event and is valid for one week for that event. It is not a lifetime purchase that covers every invitation you make.

> Premium is valid for 1 week per event — not a lifetime purchase.

### How many guest responses are allowed on the Free plan?

The Free plan allows up to 25 RSVP responses per event. Hosts can upgrade to Premium if they need unlimited responses.

> Up to 25 RSVP responses

### Can I change the time or venue after I've sent the invitation?

Yes. You edit the invitation itself, and everyone who opens the link sees the new details. You don't have to send a correction to the whole guest list.

> When a detail changes, you edit the invitation rather than sending a correction to a guest list you have to reconstruct.

### Do my guests need an account or an app to reply?

No. The invitation is a web page that opens in the browser when a guest taps your link. They don't sign up and they don't install anything.

> Guests do not create an account, and they do not install anything.

### Can I see who has replied so far?

Yes. Your dashboard updates in real time with guest counts.

> Your dashboard updates in real time with guest counts.

### Can guests add a plus-one and a meal preference when they reply?

Yes. Guests reply right away and can include plus-ones and meal preferences.

> Guests reply instantly — with plus-ones and meal preferences.

### How much does Premium cost?

Premium is $2.99 per event. The Free plan is $0 per event.

> $2.99per event

### How many templates are free?

Four of the nine templates are free. The rest come with Premium.

> four of the nine templates are free

### Can guests add the event to their calendar?

Yes. Guests can add the date to their calendar and open directions to the venue from the invitation.

> Guests can add the date to their calendar and open directions to the venue from the invitation itself

### Can I export the guest list?

Yes. You can export the list of replies from your dashboard, for example for the caterer.

> export the list when the caterer asks

### Is the Premium plan a monthly subscription?

No. Premium is a single $2.99 payment for one event, and nothing renews automatically.

> One-time payment. No subscription and no auto-renewal.

### Can I send the invitation in a language other than English?

Yes. Invitations can be created and shared in 19 languages, including right-to-left ones such as Arabic and Hebrew.

> Create and share invitations in 19 languages.

## What it does not do

- It doesn't collect more than 25 RSVP responses on the Free plan.

  > Up to 25 RSVP responses

- It doesn't offer lifetime access when upgrading to Premium for an event.

  > Premium is valid for 1 week per event — not a lifetime purchase.
